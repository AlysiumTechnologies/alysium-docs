# Alysium documentation context

Alysium lets people create AI agents, give them instructions and knowledge, choose the visitor chat's appearance, test them and publish or share them. The help library also explains conversations, analytics, plans, AgentHub and selling.

The audience includes people with no AI-agent-building experience. Their first learning journey creates a fictional café FAQ agent, adds a short reference note and tries a conversation. Sharing is optional. Existing users need direct task guides and useful recovery, without repeated obvious prerequisites.

Use the same approved consumer guides for this site and Brandy's knowledge. Consumer-facing availability matters more than backend plumbing: current spaces have only their owner, and self-service invitations/member management are unavailable.

The owner chose native Mintlify UI, ongoing free hosting, readable public HTML, smooth task navigation, search and bright Alysium branding. Public search discovery supports the owner's go-to-market hypothesis; search ranking and a business effect are not established claims.
