Alysium brings an agent's instructions, reference knowledge, visitor appearance and sharing into one app. You can develop an assistant for a particular job, try its answers and prepare the chat people will use around the same agent.

For example, a workshop FAQ assistant needs approved preparation notes, instructions for answering questions and a way for attendees to reach it. Alysium connects those parts of the work.

## One agent, from draft to visitor chat

Open [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces) and select **Build** on the agent you want to edit. Its builder has three main areas:

| Area | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Build** | Set the agent's job and answer style, choose its model, add reference knowledge and try its replies. |
| **Design** | Choose how the visitor chat looks and which conversation features it offers. |
| **Deploy** | Make the agent available and choose how people reach it. |

Build fields such as the name and instructions save automatically. Design changes use **Save Changes**. This lets you develop a draft and review it before making it available to visitors. The [practice project](/about-alysium/how-to-get-started) takes you through that whole journey.

## Decide what the agent should answer from

**Instructions** tell the agent whom to help, what to do, how to answer and where its job ends. **Knowledge** supplies reference information. For the workshop assistant, instructions might ask for short answers and a contact route when information is missing; knowledge would contain the actual workshop preparation notes.

In Build's **Settings** view, scroll to **Knowledge** to add files, pasted text, material from Library or a website source. Library stores reusable material; you choose which sources an agent can use. Website content becomes saved knowledge that can be refreshed. It does not give the agent unrestricted live browsing during every conversation.

The knowledge behavior choices guide how closely answers should rely on those sources and how to respond to a gap. You still need to compare replies with the material you supplied. You can [choose the source and attachment scope](/knowledge-and-library/give-your-agent-knowledge) and [adjust how answers use it](/knowledge-and-library/control-how-your-agent-uses-its-knowledge) as the job becomes clearer.

## Choose how people reach it

A **standalone link** opens the agent's visitor chat directly; a QR code provides another way to open that link. A **website widget** puts the chat on a site you manage. **Agenthub** is where people browse creators' available offers.

In Deploy, **Share link + QR**, **Embed on a website** and **Share & Sell** serve these different distribution tasks. A saved draft, a live public agent and an AgentHub offer have separate requirements. Making an agent live does not install it on your website or complete a paid offer.

You can [share the generated link](/share-and-deploy/share-your-agent-with-a-link-and-qr-code) with your own audience or [install the widget on your website](/share-and-deploy/put-your-agent-on-your-website).

## Compare against your requirements

Start with the task, the information the agent needs and the people who will use it. A narrow FAQ or guided discussion is easier to evaluate than an assistant expected to manage an undocumented business process. Writing that an agent should make bookings or issue refunds does not give it those abilities; check the [current capability limits](/help/what-alysium-cannot-do-yet) if outside actions are essential to your project.

A space holds your agents, files and plan. It currently contains only its owner; invitations and member-role workflows are unavailable. The [cost explanation](/about-alysium/what-alysium-costs) separates that space plan from subscriptions to creators' agents and additional AI usage.

Choose sources appropriate for the agent's audience and consider who can review its retained chats. The [privacy explanation](/about-alysium/is-my-data-safe-with-alysium) describes the available choices so you can assess a specific requirement before adding sensitive material.
