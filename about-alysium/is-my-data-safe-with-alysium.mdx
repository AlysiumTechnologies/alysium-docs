An agent uses messages and relevant reference material to produce replies. Its creator can review retained conversations with it. Before adding information, consider who will use the agent and who may review its chats.

Alysium's optional app analytics, visitor conversation history and data removal have separate controls and purposes. Understanding those differences helps you choose what to share.

## Choose what to share

When you build an agent, give it material appropriate for its intended audience. Approved workshop preparation notes may be suitable for attendees; private attendee records need a different decision. A public link or website widget gives visitors a way to ask the agent about the knowledge you provide.

When you use someone else's agent, do not treat a retained conversation as a notebook visible only to you. The creator may review it. Keep passwords, verification codes and full payment-card details out of chats and support requests.

You can learn with the fictional note in [the getting-started project](/about-alysium/how-to-get-started) before choosing real material.

## Main-app privacy choices control optional analytics

Open [Profile Settings](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=team), then select **Privacy**. The two choices concern different kinds of optional app analytics:

| Choice | What it concerns |
|---|---|
| **Product analytics** | Limited feature-use events linked to an opaque account or device ID. Chat and prompt text, AI answers, file contents, filenames, email addresses, names and full URLs are excluded. |
| **Session Replay** | A masked view of movement through approved parts of the app for investigating usability problems. It stays off unless you turn it on. |

Use each switch for your choice. The controls save the change without a separate Save button and show the saved choice alongside what is currently running. Reopen Privacy to check them. Turning off Product analytics also stops Session Replay; it does not delete conversations or hide retained chats from an agent's creator.

The [privacy-settings guide](/getting-started/choose-your-privacy-settings) explains the saved and effective states in more detail.

## Visitor chats have separate disclosures

In an agent's website widget, open **Account**, then **Data & Privacy**. Read the information for that agent's model, creator sharing and conversation retention. These visitor menus are separate from the main app's Profile Settings.

**Conversation history** controls whether future visitor messages are saved for resuming later. Turning it off does not delete previously saved history. Check the agent's retention information and the [visitor privacy choices](/widget-visitors/visitor-data-and-privacy) before deciding what to share. The [saved-history explanation](/widget-visitors/visitor-accounts-and-saved-history) covers that separate setting.

## Data removal is a separate task

Turning analytics off, logging out or detaching one knowledge source does not delete an account or all its data.

For an account-wide data or deletion request, email [support@alysium.ai](mailto:support@alysium.ai) from the address associated with your account. Describe the information or account you want removed and the scope of your request. The [account-data guide](/getting-started/delete-your-account-or-your-data) explains what to include and how to check what the team completed.

Individual Library files have a separate [Trash and permanent-deletion workflow](/knowledge-and-library/restore-or-permanently-delete-from-trash). Moving a file to Trash and erasing it are different actions; read the confirmation for the material you intend to remove and check the result.

## Ask about your specific requirement

If your project needs a particular confidentiality commitment, certification or handling rule, describe that requirement to [support@alysium.ai](mailto:support@alysium.ai) before adding the material. Include the intended use and the kind of information involved without sending the sensitive contents themselves.

You can also read Alysium's [Privacy Policy](https://www.alysium.ai/privacy-policy). For an account-specific question, [contact a person](/help/get-help-from-a-person) who can address that request.
