You can prepare an Alysium agent for a paid offer, but creating the agent is only the first part. Public access, the way you offer it and payment readiness each have their own requirements.

Start by building an agent with reliable instructions and knowledge, then check its behavior. Use this page to choose the detailed selling guides for your intended offer.

## Decide how people will reach the agent

From [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces), open the intended agent and select **Deploy**. A direct link, a website widget and an AgentHub listing are different ways to reach people.

[Ways to share or sell your agent](/selling-and-payouts/ways-to-share-or-sell-your-agent) explains the choices. Use [List your agent on Agenthub](/selling-and-payouts/list-your-agent-on-agenthub) when you want a storefront listing.

For example, a fictional workshop could share a free FAQ link while preparing a separate paid training-agent offer. Making the FAQ live would not complete the paid offer's setup.

## Find your creator setup

Your space currently contains only you, its owner. Invitations and member-role workflows are unavailable.

To find the creator area:

1. Open [Team settings](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=team).
2. Select **Creator** in **Profile Settings**.
3. Read the setup state shown there.

If Profile Settings is already open, select **Creator** and continue with step 3. The setup state determines which actions are available. [Use your Creator Dashboard](/selling-and-payouts/use-your-creator-dashboard) explains those screens. [Set your public creator handle](/spaces/set-your-public-creator-handle) covers your space's public identity.

## Prepare the paid offer

Use the guides that apply to your chosen channel:

- [Set up Stripe to get paid](/selling-and-payouts/set-up-stripe-to-get-paid) explains payment setup.
- [Price your agent](/selling-and-payouts/price-your-agent) explains the offer's price and included usage.
- [Apply to sell on Agenthub](/selling-and-payouts/apply-to-sell-on-agenthub) covers the marketplace application.

Follow the readiness messages shown for your account. Available setup controls do not mean every account can activate paid selling immediately.

## Know what is ready before offering it to customers

A **Live** state confirms the agent's publication and public access. A marketplace listing and paid offer have their own readiness checks. Receiving earnings also has separate payout rules, explained in [When and how you get paid](/selling-and-payouts/when-and-how-you-get-paid).

Check that the intended channel and paid offer are available before promising access to customers.

If setup is blocked, read the specific readiness message and follow its named guide. If **Creator** is missing or a paid activation or listing problem continues, use [Get help from a person](/help/get-help-from-a-person). Include the agent, intended channel and exact message. Keep payment credentials out of the request.
