<span id="what-alysium-costs" />

Alysium has Free and Pro plans for your space. A paid subscription to a creator's agent is a separate purchase, and enabled additional AI usage can add charges. To understand a bill or a new offer, check which of these three parts it concerns.

Use the price, billing interval and payment terms shown in the relevant offer and final checkout before paying.

## Your space plan

A space holds your agents, files and usage allowance. **Free** has no recurring space-plan fee and provides a limited allowance. **Pro** is a recurring paid space plan with its own included allowance and plan benefits.

Open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing) and check the space name, current plan, subscription status and displayed price. An existing active plan offers **Manage Subscription & Billing** for its payment details and billing history. If you see **Subscription Details Unavailable**, Alysium could not confirm the plan: reopen Billing or use **Open Billing Portal** to inspect the existing subscription before starting another purchase.

AI usage is measured in **credits**. A credit allowance is not a fixed number of messages: consumption varies with the model and the amount of input and output. To inspect recorded usage, open [Profile Settings](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=team), select **Consumption** and check the relevant period.

You can [compare Free and Pro allowances](/plans-and-billing/free-and-pro-plans-compared) or learn [how credits measure usage](/plans-and-billing/how-credits-work) before changing plans.

<span id="a-creators-agent-subscription" />

## A creator's agent subscription

A creator can charge a recurring fee for an individual agent and include a bundle of premium usage. Read the agent's offer and checkout terms for its price, included usage and access. Pro does not include every paid agent in AgentHub, and an agent subscription does not replace your space plan.

To check an existing purchase, open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing) and find the agent under **Agent Access and Subscriptions**. Read its subscription or access status, recorded price and usage. A current listing can show a different offer for new purchases; use your existing subscription details to understand your own recurring fee.

An agent bundle and your space's allowance are separate. When the bundle is exhausted, further usage depends on your space plan and its limits. Subscription renewal and the bundle's scheduled reset can also have different dates; use the dates and status shown for that purchase.

You can [review the purchase steps](/agenthub/subscribe-to-an-agent) before subscribing or [manage existing agent access](/plans-and-billing/manage-your-agent-subscriptions) afterward.

## Extra usage beyond an allowance

An active Pro subscription can allow paid AI usage after the included credits run out. This is **On-Demand Usage** in Billing, also called overages. It is billed after use, in addition to the recurring plan fee.

The saved **Billing Period Limit** controls this additional spending:

| Setting | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **Disabled** | No on-demand usage after the included credits are spent. |
| **Fixed** | Additional usage up to a USD amount for the billing period. |
| **Unlimited** | Additional usage without a fixed dollar cap. |

To change it, open **On-Demand Usage** in [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing), choose the mode beside **Billing Period Limit**, and enter a valid nonnegative USD amount for **Fixed**. Select **Save**, wait for the success message, then reopen Billing to check the saved mode and amount.

Unlimited does not add free credits. Lowering a cap does not erase charges already incurred, and usage overages do not add document-storage capacity. You can [set an additional-usage cap](/plans-and-billing/turn-on-overages-and-set-a-cap) with the full setting and recovery details.

## Ask about a specific charge

First identify the affected space plan or agent subscription in Billing and check its recorded usage and dates. If the charge still needs explanation, email [billing@alysium.ai](mailto:billing@alysium.ai) with the space or agent name, charge amount, date or reference, and what you expected. Keep passwords and full card details out of the message.

Brandy can explain the documented process. A person must handle questions about private billing records, account changes or a refund decision; [contact the billing team](/help/get-help-from-a-person) for those questions.
