Alysium has Free and Pro space plans. A subscription to a creator's agent is a separate purchase, and enabled extra usage can add charges.

To understand what you may pay, check these three parts separately. Use the offer and checkout summary shown to you for the actual price.

| Part | What to check |
|---|---|
| Space plan | The Free or Pro plan, its price and included allowances. |
| Creator-agent subscription | The agent's recurring price, included usage and offer terms. |
| Extra usage | Whether overages are enabled and what cap applies. |

## Your space plan

A space plan covers the space's platform use. Open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing) to see the plan and offer for your current space. To inspect usage, open **Profile Settings** from the app sidebar and select **Consumption**.

[Free and Pro plans compared](/plans-and-billing/free-and-pro-plans-compared) explains the allowances. [How credits work](/plans-and-billing/how-credits-work) explains how usage draws on credits.

Opening Billing does not buy Pro. You need the appropriate billing authority to change a plan. Review the displayed terms before choosing to change it.

## A creator's agent subscription

An agent's offer has its own recurring price, included usage and terms. Check these on the offer before subscribing. Upgrading your space to Pro does not mean every paid agent in AgentHub is included.

Use [Subscribe to an agent](/agenthub/subscribe-to-an-agent) for the purchase steps. To find subscriptions you already have, follow [Manage your agent subscriptions](/plans-and-billing/manage-your-agent-subscriptions).

## Extra usage beyond an allowance

Overage settings and the cap affect possible additional usage charges. They do not add file-storage capacity. Read [Turn on overages and set a cap](/plans-and-billing/turn-on-overages-and-set-a-cap) before enabling this option.

Keep this decision separate from choosing a monthly plan or subscribing to a creator's agent. You do not need to enable a chargeable option simply to follow [How to get started](/about-alysium/how-to-get-started).

## A question about a specific charge

Contact [billing@alysium.ai](mailto:billing@alysium.ai) with your account address, the charge and date, and what you expected. Keep full card details out of the request. [Get help from a person](/help/get-help-from-a-person) explains what else is useful.

A help agent can explain the process. It cannot inspect your private bill or confirm a refund; use the account-specific response for those questions.
