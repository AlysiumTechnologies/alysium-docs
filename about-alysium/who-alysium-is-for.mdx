<span id="who-alysium-is-for" />

Alysium is useful when you want an AI assistant for a defined conversational job: explaining material you supply, asking useful questions or helping someone organize their thinking. You can build your own agent or use one offered by a creator.

You choose the job and check whether the replies serve it. Starting with a small task makes that judgment easier while you learn how instructions, knowledge and the model work together.

## You have information people repeatedly ask about

An information agent can give people another way to explore an FAQ, preparation notes or reference documents. A fictional workshop owner might provide class notes and approved policies so attendees can ask what to bring or where to find material.

This is a useful first project when you can supply a clear source and check the important answers yourself. Put the facts in **Knowledge** and use **Agent Instructions** to explain how the agent should answer, including whom to contact when information is missing. Keeping those roles separate helps you correct a missing fact without rewriting the agent's whole job.

In the builder's **Build → Settings** view, scroll to **Knowledge** to add reference material. You can [choose between documents, pasted notes, Library attachments and website content](/knowledge-and-library/give-your-agent-knowledge).

## You want a structured conversation

You can give an agent a conversational method, such as asking about a goal, exploring alternatives and helping the person prepare a draft plan. Instructions describe the role and approach; reusable skills can add methods and reference material.

For example, a planning helper could ask you to explain a proposed project and help you identify questions to investigate. You review the resulting plan and decide what to do with it.

If the job depends on appointments, customer-account changes or live-web research, assess those requirements separately. Instructions and uploaded files do not connect the agent to outside services. The [current capability limits](/help/what-alysium-cannot-do-yet) explain those boundaries.

## You want to try an agent before building one

Open [AgentHub](https://app.alysium.ai/agenthub) and inspect an agent relevant to your task. Read what it is intended to do and the access or subscription terms shown on its page. Free previews, installation choices and editing rights depend on the particular offer.

Trying an offered agent can help you decide what kind of conversation you want from your own. It does not automatically give you access to that creator's builder. You can [assess an agent's page and offer](/agenthub/read-an-agents-page) before choosing to use it.

## You want other people to use your agent

You can prepare an agent for visitors through a standalone chat link or a widget installed on a website you manage. A paid offer and an AgentHub listing have their own setup and requirements.

Open [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces), select **Build** on your agent's card and use the builder's **Build**, **Design** and **Deploy** areas to develop, present and share it. Your space holds the agents, files and plan; sharing visitor chat does not invite someone into that space. Spaces currently contain only their owner.

You can [compare the ways to offer an agent](/about-alysium/selling-agents-with-alysium), or [learn by making a complete practice agent](/about-alysium/how-to-get-started).

## Check the fit for sensitive information

Consider the audience who will use the agent, the material you can share with them and who can review retained conversations. Choose a job with sources and important results you can check. The [privacy explanation](/about-alysium/is-my-data-safe-with-alysium) describes those boundaries and the separate optional analytics choices.

For a sensitive or regulated use, ask [support@alysium.ai](mailto:support@alysium.ai) about the specific requirement before relying on Alysium for it. Describe what information or access the project needs, without sending the sensitive material itself.
