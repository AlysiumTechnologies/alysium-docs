<span id="get-a-refund-for-an-agent-subscription" />

Ask Alysium billing to review a specific agent-subscription payment. Send the actual charge details and explain what went wrong so the request can be matched to the right purchase.

If you only want to stop the next renewal, [cancel the subscription](/agenthub/cancel-an-agent-subscription). Ordinary cancellation keeps access through the current paid period. A refund request asks for money back and needs a separate review.

## Identify the charge

1. Open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing) and check the space name. Billing opens your current space's settings.
2. Scroll to **Agent Access and Subscriptions**, below the space's plan and usage settings. Find the agent and match its creator name.
3. Note the subscription card's price and status. Use your payment confirmation or invoice to find the actual payment date, amount and reference for the charge you want reviewed.

Use the payment record for the amount you paid; the listing's current offer may differ from that charge.

## Request a review of the payment

Email [billing@alysium.ai](mailto:billing@alysium.ai) with:

- Your space name and the agent and creator names.
- The payment date and amount.
- The payment confirmation or invoice reference.
- What happened and why you are requesting a refund.
- Whether you also want future renewal stopped.

For example, explain the task you tried and the problem you encountered, using the date and amount from your payment record. State the renewal request separately so both requests are clear. Include the payment reference rather than a complete card number.

Keep the sent request and any replies or refund confirmation. Sending the request begins a review; it does not confirm approval.

## Understand eligibility and a pending result

Refunds are reviewed payment by payment. The creator's in-app refund window is 30 days for eligible successful charges; older charges need support review. A request within that window is not a guarantee of approval.

A confirmed full eligible refund of the payment funding your current service period can cause immediate cancellation. Access ends when Stripe confirms that cancellation. A partial refund, a refund for an older period, or a request that is pending, failed or still under review does not by itself establish that your current access has ended.

Follow the payment status and confirmation provided for your request. A submitted or pending refund is different from money received by your bank.

After a confirmed refund, reopen [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing), scroll to **Agent Access and Subscriptions** and check the agent's status. If the refund confirmation and subscription state conflict, reply to the same billing request with the confirmation reference and the status you see.
