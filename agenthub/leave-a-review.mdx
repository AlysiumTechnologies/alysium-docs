Share your experience on an AgentHub listing, then edit or remove your own review when needed. A marketplace review is public and differs from [feedback on one chat answer](/share-and-deploy/rate-an-answer).

Read the listing's purpose and try the agent before describing how well it helped. Keep private conversation content out of a public review.

## Open Reviews

**Already in the listing's Reviews section?** Continue with [Give a star rating](#give-a-star-rating), or [Add or change your comment](#add-or-change-your-comment) if your review is already saved.

1. Open [AgentHub](https://app.alysium.ai/agenthub).
2. Select the agent you want to review.
3. Expand **Reviews**.
4. Continue with [Give a star rating](#give-a-star-rating).

AgentHub opens the marketplace; you select the listing there. Posting requires a named account. If prompted, sign in or select **Create free account**, then return to that listing's **Reviews**. See [Create an Alysium account](/getting-started/create-an-alysium-account) if you need account help.

## Give a star rating

Selecting stars can save a rating immediately. The form can change after that rating-only save, so check what is already saved before submitting again.

1. Select the star rating that reflects your experience.
2. Wait for any save confirmation.
3. Check whether **Your Review** appears with that rating.
4. Continue with [Add or change your comment](#add-or-change-your-comment) if you want to include text.

## Add or change your comment

If **Your Review** is already shown:

1. Select **Edit** on your own review.
2. Change the stars or comment as needed.
3. Select **Save Changes**.
4. Check the saved rating and text.

**Cancel** leaves the saved version unchanged.

If the new-review form is still shown:

1. Enter your experience in the review-comment field.
2. Select **Submit Review**.
3. Check that your review appears with the intended rating and text.

Typing a comment alone does not confirm that it was saved.

For a fictional writing assistant, a review might say: “It helped me turn a long announcement into a clearer customer message. I still checked the dates before sending it.” Replace this with your actual experience.

## Remove your review

Deleting a review cannot be undone. Find your own saved review in the listing's **Reviews** section before continuing.

1. Select **Delete** on your review.
2. Read the **Delete Review** confirmation.

Select **Cancel** to keep it. To remove it:

1. Select **Delete Review** in the confirmation.
2. Wait for your review to disappear.

## If the review does not save

Check that you are using a named account. Select a rating and supply a comment when the displayed form requires one. Read any error, then check your saved review before submitting again.

A marketplace review does not change your subscription, stop renewal or request a refund. Use [Cancel an agent subscription](/agenthub/cancel-an-agent-subscription) or [Get a refund for an agent subscription](/agenthub/get-a-refund-for-an-agent-subscription) for those tasks.
