Share a public star rating and, if you want, a comment about how an AgentHub agent helped you. You can later change or delete your own review. This reviews the agent as a whole; feedback on an individual chat answer is separate.

## Open Reviews

Open [AgentHub](https://app.alysium.ai/agenthub) and select the agent's card. **Reviews** is an expandable section below the listing's main details, pricing and preview area. Select it to read published reviews or find **Your Review** if you have already posted one.

You can give an initial rating after adding a free agent to your space or having an active subscription to a paid agent. The entry point is the listing's **How would you rate this agent?** prompt. The summary stars beside the agent's name show its average rating.

## Give a star rating

1. In **How would you rate this agent?**, choose one to five stars to reflect your experience.
2. Check **Your Review** in the expanded **Reviews** section for the rating you chose. If needed, reopen the listing and expand Reviews to check the saved result.

Choosing a star can save a rating immediately without a comment. Check the saved review before submitting again or adding text.

## Add or change your comment

To add a comment to a saved rating, or change a review:

1. In **Reviews**, find **Your Review** and select **Edit**.
2. In **Edit Your Review**, change **Update rating:** and/or write in **Updated review comment**.
3. Select **Save Changes** and check that your saved stars and comment show the intended changes.

Select **Cancel** to leave the saved version unchanged.

Describe the task you tried, what helped and any limits you encountered. For example: “It helped me turn a long announcement into a clearer customer message. I still checked the dates before sending it.” Use your own experience and keep private conversation details out of the public comment.

If **Share Your Experience** is still shown as a new-review form, enter a nonblank **Review comment**, then select **Submit Review**. Check that **Your Review** appears with the intended rating and text. Typing a comment alone does not save it.

## Remove your review

Deleting a review is permanent.

1. Expand **Reviews**, find **Your Review** and select **Edit**.
2. Select **Delete** inside the editing form.
3. Read the **Delete Review** confirmation. Select **Cancel** to keep the review, or **Delete Review** to remove it.
4. Check for **Your review has been deleted.** and confirm that your review disappears.

## If the review does not save

Read any error displayed inside **Reviews**. Check the saved review before trying again, including by reopening the listing if the result is unclear. Correct any required input shown by the form, then repeat the action only if the intended review has not saved. **Dismiss error** closes the displayed error message.

If Reviews shows **Create a free account to rate and review this agent.**, select **Create free account** to create an account, then return to the listing.
