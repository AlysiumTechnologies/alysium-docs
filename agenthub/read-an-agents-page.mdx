An AgentHub listing helps you decide whether an agent fits your task and whether its offer is right for you. Open [AgentHub](https://app.alysium.ai/agenthub) and select an agent card to see its full page.

## Purpose and creator

Check the agent's name, the creator named after **by**, and the short description. The header also shows a user count and any average rating and review count.

Expand **About this agent** to read the longer description and **Best used for:** capabilities. Match these to the job you want help with. For example, a writing assistant that helps draft customer replies may fit a different need from one that edits long reports.

## Price, allowance and usage

Read the price and access details beside the main content on a wide screen, or below it on a smaller screen.

| Offer detail | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Free / No subscription required** | There is no recurring subscription fee for access to this agent. Your space's usage allowance and limits still matter when chatting. |
| Monthly price and **Billed monthly · cancel anytime** | The recurring fee for access to a paid agent. Check the offer again in checkout before paying. |
| Included credits, when shown | An allowance for AI usage, rather than a fixed number of messages. Not every paid offer includes an allowance. |
| Premium usage-rate notice, when shown | The agent uses an above-standard usage rate. For a paid offer with a **Premium Allowance**, that allowance is spent first; after it runs out, the premium rate draws from your space's balance. |

An individual agent subscription is separate from Alysium Pro. Your space plan and its limits continue to apply alongside the agent's offer.

## Available actions

The main button depends on the offer and your existing access.

| Control | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Subscribe** | Opens checkout for a paid agent. [Review the offer and confirm access](/agenthub/subscribe-to-an-agent) before treating it as an active subscription. |
| **Add to My Agents** | Adds free access to your current space. You can then [find the installed agent](/agenthub/add-a-free-agent-to-your-space). |
| **Add Copy** | Appears on an already-added free listing. It uses the add flow again and can return an existing installation, so it does not guarantee another separate copy. |
| **Chat Now** | Opens your installed agent with creator-managed behavior. |
| **Customize your agent** | Opens your editable copy in the builder, where you can adjust its setup and knowledge. |
| **You own this agent** | Identifies your own listing; the button is disabled. Open the source agent from [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces). |

An installed agent can offer **Chat Now** or **Customize your agent** depending on the kind of copy you acquired. Editable copies have their own setup and knowledge; access to one agent does not make every listing editable.

While the page shows **Checking agent access…**, wait for the access check. If the check fails, use **Retry agent access** and check the resulting button.

Before subscribing to a paid agent you do not own, you can [try its limited preview](/agenthub/try-an-agent-for-free) in **Try [agent name] free — limited preview**. The preview has a limited allowance and can stop accepting messages when that allowance is spent.

## Reviews and images

Expand **Reviews** to read other people's experiences. Consider the number of reviews alongside the average rating: a small count gives you fewer experiences to compare. After using an installed agent, you can [leave your own review](/agenthub/leave-a-review).

When the creator has supplied images, use **View full image** to open one. Select **Close image** or press Escape to close it. With multiple images, use **Previous image** and **Next image**, or the left and right arrow keys, to move through them.

## Technical details

Expand **Technical Details** to see **Model**, **Current Version**, **Version Date** and **Created**. These details can help you compare listings or identify the version you are asking about.
