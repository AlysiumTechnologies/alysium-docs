Subscribe to a paid AgentHub agent for your space, then open the installed agent when access is confirmed. The agent subscription is separate from your space's Alysium Pro plan.

## Open checkout

Start in the space where you want the agent installed. You can return to [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces) to check your current space before buying.

1. Open [AgentHub](https://app.alysium.ai/agenthub) and select the paid agent you want.
2. Read its purpose, creator and price, then select **Subscribe**.

A dialog headed **Subscribe to [agent name]** opens on the page with the offer and a secure payment form. If an account screen opens instead, complete the offered flow. When you return to the agent's listing, select **Subscribe** again to open the payment form.

## Review the offer and pay

Check the agent and creator names, monthly price, included credits when shown, and any Premium usage-rate explanation. **Billed monthly · cancel anytime** means this is a recurring subscription.

The agent's **Premium Allowance** is separate from your space's own credit allowance. The agent's included allowance is used first; further usage depends on your space's plan and limits. Subscribing to an agent does not upgrade the space to Pro. You can [read how credits and allowances work](/plans-and-billing/how-credits-work) if you need to compare the two.

1. Enter the payment information requested in the form.
2. Select **Subscribe · [displayed price]/mo**, or **Subscribe** when the button has no price.
3. Complete any payment verification requested. Wait while the form shows **Processing…** or **Confirming…**.
4. Look for **You're subscribed**, which confirms access and shows the recurring price and included credits.

Entering payment details or seeing a pending purchase record does not confirm active access.

## Start using the installed agent

The confirmation offers an action for the copy installed in your space:

- **Start chatting** opens a managed agent's chat. Type a question in the message box and select **Send message**, or press Enter. Check the reply against the job you want the agent to do.
- **Customize your agent** opens the builder when the installed copy allows editing. You can review and change that copy's setup in your space.

A managed agent lets you chat while keeping its setup locked. When you return to its listing, **Chat Now** opens the installed chat; an editable copy offers **Customize your agent** instead.

To check the saved subscription, open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing) and scroll to **Agent Access and Subscriptions**. Match the agent and creator names, then read the status, renewal details and allowance. Billing shows records for your active space. You can [manage the subscription there](/plans-and-billing/manage-your-agent-subscriptions), including stopping a future renewal.

## Check pending payment or missing access

If the payment form cannot prepare checkout and offers **Try again**, use it. For a payment error or verification request, follow the message in the form.

If payment succeeded but Alysium is still confirming access, keep the confirmation and check the existing record in **Billing** → **Agent Access and Subscriptions** before starting another purchase. On the listing, wait for **Checking agent access…**; use **Retry agent access** if offered.

If a confirmed payment still has no access, email [billing@alysium.ai](mailto:billing@alysium.ai) with the agent name, displayed status and payment confirmation reference.
