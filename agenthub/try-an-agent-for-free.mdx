Try a short request in a paid agent's listing before deciding whether to subscribe. This is a limited preview; its available messages depend on the agent's offer.

## Find the offered preview

Open [AgentHub](https://app.alysium.ai/agenthub) and select a paid agent you have not purchased. Read what it is designed to help with, then scroll to **Try [agent name] free — limited preview**. The preview chat is on the listing itself.

## Try a relevant question

Give the agent one small task related to its stated job. Include everything it needs in the request so you can judge the answer on its own.

For a writing agent, you might try:

> Rewrite this customer notice in a warm, clear tone. Keep it to two sentences and preserve the dates and opening time: “We are closed Monday 12 May for maintenance. We reopen Tuesday 13 May at 9 am.”

1. Enter your request in the preview's message box.
2. Select **Send message**, or press Enter.
3. Wait for the answer, then compare it with your request. In the example, check the tone, length, dates and time.

After a successful message, the chat may show a remaining-message counter, **Last free message** or **Free trial ended**. A counter does not have to appear before your first reply, and there is no single preview message count for every agent.

The sample reply helps you decide whether the agent suits your task. There is no agent setup to save or install during this preview.

## When preview messages are spent

You can stop after trying the agent. Sending a preview message does not subscribe you or charge you for a subscription.

If you want to review paid access, select **See plans** or **Upgrade** when offered in the chat, or **Subscribe** on the listing. This opens the subscription offer. Check its monthly fee, included allowance and usage-rate explanation before deciding whether to pay. If you decide to subscribe, [complete the payment form and check access confirmation](/agenthub/subscribe-to-an-agent).

## If the preview cannot send

Read the notice in the chat. If the free trial has ended, you can review the paid offer or stop. If the notice says the agent is temporarily unavailable for a free preview, the creator cannot currently fund preview use; the notice can still offer a subscription. You can leave the preview without buying.

If you already subscribe, open the installed agent rather than judging paid access from the public preview. Return to its AgentHub listing and select **Chat Now**, or **Customize your agent** for an editable copy. To check the subscription, open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing), scroll to **Agent Access and Subscriptions**, and match the agent and creator names. The public preview and your paid allowance are separate.
