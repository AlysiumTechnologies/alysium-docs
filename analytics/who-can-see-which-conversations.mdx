<span id="who-can-see-which-conversations" />

As the owner of a space, you can read its agents' saved conversations in **Analytics**, including conversations with visitors. Someone who chats with your agent can see their own saved chats with it; that does not give them your Analytics view or other visitors' conversations.

The same applies when you use another person's agent. Being able to chat with it does not give you access to its Analytics.

<span id="chat-access-and-analytics-access-are-different" />

## Read your agent's saved conversations

Open [Analytics](https://app.alysium.ai/analytics), choose your agent under **Your Agents** and select a conversation under **Recent Conversations**. Wait for its messages to load. On a small screen, choose a conversation in **Chats**, then open **Msgs** to read it.

The agent's [activity page](/analytics/see-one-agents-analytics) also includes visitor summaries. Those summaries and the conversation list can count different things, especially when you include your own chats in the list.

<span id="why-your-own-practice-chat-can-be-missing" />

## Why one of your own chats may be hidden

Your own saved chats can be hidden when the list is focused on visitors. When **Show teammate conversations** appears below the list controls, turn it on to include your own chats.

Despite its name, this setting includes you, the owner. Your space currently contains only you; you do not need another member to use it.

The setting follows the account recorded for that particular conversation. A chat on a website can use a separate visitor session even while you are signed into the Alysium app. Being signed into the app alone does not classify every website or preview chat as one of your owner conversations.

Including your chats changes the list, its count and the conversations searched. It does not add your chats to visitor **People** or other visitor summary metrics.

<span id="a-filter-can-hide-a-conversation-you-can-read" />

## Why another conversation may be missing

First check which agent is selected. Clear an unintended search or adjust the date range to find the saved chat you want.

A loading error is different from an empty history. If the list says **Couldn't load these conversations**, select **Retry** and wait for the result. If the wrong agents appear, open [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces) and check the account and active space.

There is also a limit to what can appear in saved history. Visitors can turn off **Conversation history** in their chat settings. Messages sent with history off are not added to ordinary saved conversation history, so changing Analytics filters cannot reveal those messages. Turning history off controls saving new messages; it does not delete older saved chats. Visitors can [learn how saved history works](/widget-visitors/visitor-accounts-and-saved-history) for their own conversations.

<span id="a-downloaded-copy-has-a-different-audience" />

## What happens when you download a conversation

Downloading creates a separate file on your device. Anyone you send that file to can read the copy, even if they cannot open the original conversation in Analytics.

**Export → Download Conversation** downloads the selected exchange. **Bulk Download** includes accessible conversations with messages for the selected agent and leaves out empty conversations. It uses its own selection of history, ignoring the list's current search, date range and **Show teammate conversations** setting.

Before sharing a bulk file, open it and check which conversations it contains. If you want to share particular exchanges, [download them individually](/analytics/export-conversations) instead.
