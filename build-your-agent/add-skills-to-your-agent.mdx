<span id="add-skills-to-your-agent" />

A skill gives your agent reusable instructions for a kind of work, such as organizing an answer or following a writing method. Knowledge supplies the facts the agent should draw on. Choose a skill whose method supports your agent's job, then add it to your space and attach it to the agent.

## Open Skills

Open [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces). On the intended agent's card, select **Build**. Select **Settings** if another builder view is open, then scroll to **Skills** in the **Agent Builder** section.

Use **Browse skill library** to choose from the catalog or your space's existing skills. The **Add skill** button in the Skills header opens the uploader for [a skill bundle of your own](/build-your-agent/upload-your-own-skill).

## Choose a skill

1. Select **Browse skill library**. Open **My Skills** to reuse something your space already holds, or browse a discovery category for other choices.
2. Enter the work you want help with in **Search skills by intent…**, or browse without a search. The **All**, **Alysium**, **Free** and **Premium** filters and the tags narrow the choices. If a search or filter hides useful options, change or clear it. A search with no matching skill offers a way to browse the library.
3. Select a skill's name or description to open its details. Read the description and **SKILL.md**, and use the file list to read any reference material that affects the task.

Look for guidance that fits the information you will supply and the answer you want. A concise FAQ assistant may benefit from a clear answer structure; a method that always produces a long sales pitch could conflict with its job. You can [review the publisher, contents and trust setting](/build-your-agent/review-a-skill-before-adding-it) before deciding.

If **Suggested** appears after Alysium drafted your agent, it contains suggestions that are not yet attached. Guided creation may already have attached other skills; the agent's Skills list shows those attachments.

## Add it to your space and attach it

Adding a catalog skill to your space makes it available in **My Skills**. Attaching it makes it available to this agent.

1. For a free skill your space does not yet hold, select **Add to my space** in its details. Wait for the action to finish.
2. When **Attach to agent** appears, select it. If your space already holds the skill, you can start with this action.

In the picker, the shorter **Add** and **Attach** controls perform these same separate actions. After using **Add**, find the skill in **My Skills** and select **Attach**, or reopen its details and select **Attach to agent**.

An unowned priced skill offers **Get** in the picker or a disabled **Purchase soon** in its details. **Get** shows that purchasing skills is coming soon; paid skill checkout is unavailable. **Premium** describes the skill's price, rather than your space's subscription plan.

## Check the result

Return to the agent's **Skills** list. The skill should have a row with its switch on. Attachment enables it, and the agent can load its guidance when a task is relevant. Check the switch for enablement: the section's active count also includes attached skills whose switch is off.

Skill actions save as you perform them. Wait for each action to finish before making another change. If an action reports an error, read the message and check both **My Skills** and the attached list before retrying. A skill in My Skills but missing from this agent needs **Attach**, rather than another **Add**.

To pause a skill, turn its row's switch off, or use **Disable on this agent** in its details. Turn it back on to restore its use. **Remove** in the row, or **Remove from agent** in the details, removes the attachment and leaves the skill in your space's My Skills for reuse.

For a first check, use the chat beside Settings, or select **Chat Preview** on a smaller screen. Enter a task that matches the skill's description and select **Send message**. Compare the reply's method with the skill's instructions. Preview conversations use your space's credits. You can [test more of the agent's behavior](/build-your-agent/test-your-agent-while-you-build) as you refine it.
