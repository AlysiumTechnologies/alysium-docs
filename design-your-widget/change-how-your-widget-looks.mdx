<span id="change-how-your-widget-looks" />

Choose a theme for your agent's visitor chat, check how its picture and text look together, and save the result.

## Open the appearance controls

Open [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces), select **Build** on the agent's card, then select the main **Design** tab. Scroll the controls to **Theme**.

On a wide screen, **Widget Preview** sits beside the controls. On a smaller screen, use the **Design** subtab for settings and **Preview** to inspect the widget.

## Choose the appearance

1. In **Theme**, select **Change** to open the theme chooser.
2. Select a named theme, such as **Light** or **Dark**, and inspect the preview. The preview changes as you choose.
3. Select **Done** to close the chooser.

Check that the greeting, starter questions and message text are easy to read. Choose a theme that works with the page where people will use the widget.

Under **Chat Window**, you can also change **Show avatars**. Check the result on the widget's Home and Chat views: turning it off can leave a letter in place of the agent's picture. To use a different picture, [upload an agent image in Build](/build-your-agent/set-your-agents-name-picture-and-description).

## Save the appearance

The preview shows your edits before they are saved.

1. On a smaller screen, return to the **Design** subtab.
2. Scroll to the save card at the top of the Design controls and select **Save Changes**.
3. Wait for **All Changes Saved**.

If an error appears on the save card, keep the page open, read the message and retry **Save Changes** when it is available. The changed preview alone does not confirm a successful save.

## Check the saved result

Reopen the same agent from [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces) and return to **Design**. Confirm that the theme and **Show avatars** setting remain as you chose them.

If you already use this widget on a website, check that agent's widget there too. If it looks different, first check that the Design save succeeded and that the website uses the intended agent.

You can also [change the chat title and typing hint](/design-your-widget/change-the-widgets-title-and-message-box-text) to help visitors understand what to ask.
