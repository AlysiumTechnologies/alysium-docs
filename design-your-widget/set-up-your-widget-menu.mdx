Give visitors a way to learn about you, read your policies and get help. The **Agent Menu** settings supply the details behind the widget's **Account** menu.

## Open Agent Menu

Open [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces), select **Build** on the intended agent's card, then open the main **Design** tab. Scroll down to **Agent Menu**.

On a smaller screen, the **Design** subtab shows the controls and **Preview** shows the widget. Switch between them as you edit and inspect the result.

## Choose the entries

Select **Terms & Privacy**, **Help & Support**, **About profile** or **Contact info** to add its editor card. The new card starts collapsed; select its heading to open the fields. Open an existing card the same way to change it.

| Setting | What visitors receive |
|---|---|
| **Terms & Privacy** | A link in Account to your public policy page, once you enter a URL. |
| **Help & Support** | A link to your external help page. Without a help URL, this menu item opens the widget's contact screen. |
| **About profile** | Your public background and links in **About This Agent**. |
| **Contact info** | Your details on the internal **Help & Support** screen. |

Choose either an external help page or the internal contact screen as the destination for **Help & Support**. If you set a help URL, that URL takes visitors directly to the external page instead of the contact screen.

## Set policy and help destinations

In **Terms & Privacy**, enter the public URL of your policy. The visitor link appears when a URL is saved.

In **Help & Support**, enter the URL of the help page you want visitors to use. Leave that card without a URL if you want the menu item to open the contact details you configure instead.

Use each card's **Remove** action to remove its configured content. **About This Agent** and **Help & Support** remain built-in menu items; removing their editor cards does not hide those destinations.

## Describe the creator in About profile

Enter **Your name** and **Your story** to explain who is behind the agent and why visitors might find it useful.

Use **+ Bio** or **+ Credential** for more background. **+ More** offers fields including LinkedIn, Twitter, Website, Space name, Space description and Unique value. The **Learn more link (optional)** field can point to a page with more information.

To share an external booking page, use **Website** or **Learn more link (optional)**. The editor also has a **Book a call** field, but that field does not currently appear in the visitor profile.

Keep these details public-facing. They describe the creator; the agent's behavior is set separately in its Build instructions.

## Set Contact info

1. Add or expand **Contact info**.
2. Enter a short introduction in **Message at top**, such as what your team can help with.
3. Enter your contact address in **Email**.
4. Use **+ Phone**, **+ Website** or **+ Discord** to add another contact route. **+ More** offers Twitter, Address and More info.

Follow the format guidance beside the fields: Website accepts a full URL or domain, Discord takes an invitation link, and Twitter takes a handle. Check any guidance that appears when you leave a field.

On the visitor contact screen, **Copy** beside an email address or phone number copies it and changes to **Copied**. The visitor then uses that detail in their own email or phone app.

## Save and inspect the menu

1. Return to the **Design** controls if you were using small-screen Preview, then scroll to the top and select **Save Changes**. Wait for **All Changes Saved**.
2. Inspect the widget preview. If it is showing an active chat, select **Back to browse**, then open **Account**. On a smaller screen, select **Preview** first.
3. Open **About This Agent**, **Help & Support** and your policy link as applicable. Check the displayed wording and where each link leads.
4. Reopen the same agent from Home and check its Agent Menu fields to confirm the saved values remain.

If the widget is already on your website, check its menu there too. The [widget preview](/design-your-widget/preview-your-widget) lets you inspect changes while editing; the installed widget shows the visitor's actual website experience.

## If an entry is missing or wrong

If **Terms & Privacy** is missing, check that its URL is filled in and that the Design save finished. For a wrong destination or contact detail, correct its field and save again.

If you expected contact details but **Help & Support** opens a website, check the Help & Support URL. Removing that URL restores the internal contact destination after saving.

If saving reports an error, keep the editor open, read the message and try **Save Changes** again when available. Reopen the same agent to check the stored result before relying on it.
