Alysium runs in your browser. Its navigation and agent builder rearrange to fit the screen: a wide screen can show settings and a chat preview beside each other, while a smaller screen puts them in separate views.

## Find controls on a smaller screen

1. Open [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces), which shows your current space.
2. If the sidebar is hidden, select **Open navigation menu** at the top of the page.
3. Choose the area you need, such as **Library**, **Agenthub** or **Profile Settings**.

To edit an agent, open its card on Home. On a phone, use the bottom dock to switch between **Build**, **Design** and **Deploy**.

Within **Build**, select **Settings** for the agent's setup, **Profile** for its identity or **Chat Preview** to try a conversation. On a wide screen, the chat preview appears beside the settings. You can [plan a broader set of preview checks](/build-your-agent/test-your-agent-while-you-build) when you are ready to test more of the agent's behavior.

## Report a device-specific failure

If a page or control fails on your device, email [Alysium support](mailto:support@alysium.ai) with:

- Your device and browser name and version.
- The screen you were using and the action you tried.
- The message or result you saw.
- Whether the same task worked on another device, if you already checked.

## If only an installed widget fails

A chat widget on another website has its own installation, access and visitor-session requirements. If the app opens but that widget does not, [find the matching widget-loading symptom](/widget-visitors/when-the-widget-will-not-load) to check that separate problem.
