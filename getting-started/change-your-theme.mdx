Use **Workspace Theme** to choose the appearance of the Alysium app in the browser you are using. You can change it from the main navigation without opening an agent.

## Open the theme choices

1. Open [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces).
2. If the sidebar is hidden, select **Open navigation menu** at the top of the page.
3. Select **Workspace Theme** to open **Choose Theme**.

## Apply a theme

Choose **Light** or **Dark** for a light or dark appearance, or try a styled theme such as **Alysium Fields**. Select the theme you prefer.

The app changes immediately and the menu closes. There is no separate **Save Changes** action. Alysium remembers the appearance in this browser.

Reopen **Workspace Theme** to see the check beside the selected theme. You can select a different one at any time.

## If the appearance is not what you expected

If the app did not change as expected, reopen **Workspace Theme**, check the marked selection and select your intended theme again. For a repeated display problem, email [Alysium support](mailto:support@alysium.ai) with your device, browser, chosen theme and what you see.

A website chat has its own appearance. To change what visitors see there, open the agent's card on [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces) and select **Design**. You can [adjust the widget's appearance](/design-your-widget/change-how-your-widget-looks) there.
