Choose whether to allow optional **Product analytics** and **Session Replay** for your use of Alysium. These are personal preferences for your own app use.

## Open Privacy

1. Open [Team settings](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=team), which opens your current space's settings on **Team**.
2. Select **Privacy** and wait for your choices to load.

You can also open **Profile Settings** from the app's sidebar, then select **Privacy**. If the sidebar is hidden, use **Open navigation menu** at the top of the page.

## Choose Product analytics

**Product analytics** shares limited feature-use events linked to an identifier for your account or device. These events exclude chat and prompt text, answers, file contents, filenames, names, email and full URLs.

Set the **Product analytics** switch according to your preference:

- **On** permits these optional feature-use events when analytics can run.
- **Off** refuses these events. It also stops Session Replay from running, while keeping your replay choice saved separately.

Turning analytics on does not give permission for Session Replay. Its permission is a separate choice.

## Choose Session Replay

**Session Replay** records a masked view of how you move through the builder, marketplace, Library and app chat. It needs your explicit permission.

Expand **What Session Replay records** before choosing. The disclosure explains that:

- Text written by you or an agent, and images, are hidden. Alysium's menu and button labels remain readable.
- Sign-in, analytics, account and billing screens, and third-party website chat widgets, are excluded.
- PostHog processes replays in the US. The Alysium team can access them, and they are deleted after 30 days.

Turn **Session Replay** on if you want to allow this recording, or off to refuse or withdraw permission. You can withdraw an allowed choice even when recording is currently unavailable. The [privacy policy](https://www.alysium.ai/privacy-policy) provides more detail about how Alysium handles data.

## Confirm the saved choice and effective state

Each switch saves automatically, without a separate **Save Changes** action. Read the badge beside it and the smaller explanation underneath:

- For **Product analytics**, the badge can show **On by default**, **On after notice** or **Off**.
- For **Session Replay**, it can show **Pending**, **Allowed**, **Not allowed** or **Withdrawn**.

The badge describes the preference. The smaller explanation tells you whether it is taking effect. For example, an **Allowed** replay choice can remain off because Product analytics is off or your browser has Do Not Track enabled. Replay also applies only in the areas listed in its disclosure.

After making your choices, leave **Privacy** and reopen it to check what it shows. If a choice does not remain as expected, email [Alysium support](mailto:support@alysium.ai) with the setting, badge and smaller explanation shown. Keep private chat contents and passwords out of the report.
