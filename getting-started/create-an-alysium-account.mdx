<span id="create-an-alysium-account" />

Create an account to build and manage your own agents. Your personal space holds your agents, files and usage allowance.

If you are already chatting as a guest in the main app, open its navigation and select **Create Free Account** in **Temporary Session**. Keep that session open while you register. This starts account creation from your guest chat.

<span id="choose-a-registration-method" />

## Choose how to register

If you came from a guest chat, use the registration form it already opened. If you are starting fresh, open [Create an account](https://app.alysium.ai/auth/signup). You can register with an email and password or use your Google account.

### Register with email and password

1. Fill in **First Name**, **Last Name** and **Email** with your own details.
2. Enter a **Password** with at least 8 characters.
3. Complete the browser verification check if shown, then select **Create an Account**.

### Register with Google

Select **Continue with Google**, choose the Google account you want to use for Alysium and follow Google's prompts. Use that same Google identity when you return to Alysium.

## Finish the step shown

Alysium may finish setup immediately or ask you to confirm your email. If you reach sign-in with an email-confirmation notice, open the message in your inbox and follow its instructions, then sign in. Check spam and the address you entered if the message is missing.

When converting a guest session, you may see **Check your email** and a **Verification code** field:

1. Check the email address displayed on the screen.
2. Get the code from the message sent to that address.
3. Enter it in **Verification code** and select **Verify and create account**.

If **Choose a password** appears, enter a password with at least 8 characters in **Password** and select **Set password**. Finish this existing step rather than starting registration again.

## Find your new space

After setup, Alysium may return you to the chat or purchase you were continuing. Open [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces) to see your active space and **Create New Agent**.

Your account and space are ready for building; account creation does not create an agent for you. You can [create your first agent](/build-your-agent/create-your-first-agent) when you are ready. If you started from a guest chat, check your available conversations after completing the transition.

## If registration does not finish

- **Create an Account is unavailable:** check that both names and a valid email are filled in, the password has at least 8 characters and browser verification has completed. If the check fails, select **Retry verification**, complete the new check and submit the form. You can [get more help with the browser check](/getting-started/fix-a-verification-check).
- **The account is already registered:** open [Sign in](https://app.alysium.ai/auth/login) and use the existing account.
- **Setup asks you to log in manually:** open Sign in and use the email and password or Google identity you just registered.
- **Google says its email is unverified:** verify the address with Google or register using email and password.

If registration still fails, email [Alysium support](mailto:support@alysium.ai) with the registration method, step you tried and exact message shown. Keep passwords and verification codes out of the request.
