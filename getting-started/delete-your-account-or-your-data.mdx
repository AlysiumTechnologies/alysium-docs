For account deletion, a copy of your data or an account-wide correction, email [support@alysium.ai](mailto:support@alysium.ai) with the outcome you want. **Profile Settings** currently has no account-deletion or account-wide export button.

Choose the scope carefully. Removing one file or agent affects that item; an account-wide request needs to say what you want to happen to the account and its data.

## Choose the scope

| What you want | What to do |
|---|---|
| Delete your account, request a copy of your data or correct account information | Email the Alysium team with the account and intended outcome. |
| Stop one agent using a source | [Remove its knowledge attachment](/knowledge-and-library/remove-a-file-from-your-agent). The saved file stays in Library. |
| Remove a saved Library file | [Use Trash or request permanent deletion](/knowledge-and-library/restore-or-permanently-delete-from-trash). Moving a file to Trash does not itself stop agents already using it; completed permanent erasure cannot be undone. |
| Remove one agent | [Delete that agent](/share-and-deploy/delete-an-agent), a separate action from deleting your account. |
| Take an agent's public link or website embed offline | [Unpublish it or make it private](/share-and-deploy/unpublish-your-agent). This does not erase its records or cancel purchases. |

Before confirming irreversible removal, identify the information you need to keep. [Conversation downloads](/analytics/export-conversations) can omit messages, so open and check any records you download. Do not rely on them as a complete account backup.

Logging out or turning optional analytics off does not delete your account.

## Send an account-level request

Email [support@alysium.ai](mailto:support@alysium.ai), using the email address associated with your Alysium account where possible. Include:

- **The outcome you want:** account deletion, a copy of your data, a correction or removal of particular content.
- **What the request concerns:** the account email and any relevant agent, file or conversation.
- **Questions about the scope:** for account deletion, ask how published content, purchases, copies and retained records will be handled before you confirm permanent removal.

For example:

> I use this email address for Alysium. I would like to delete my account. Please explain the next steps, what information you need to identify it, and how my published content, purchases, copies and retained records would be handled.

Adapt the request to your intended outcome. Do not include passwords, verification codes, access tokens, payment-card details or private document contents.

## What happens after sending

Sending the email completes the request step. It does not itself erase the account or produce a download.

Keep the team's reply and follow the next steps for your request. If the scope is unclear, ask for clarification before confirming permanent removal. Treat the work as completed only when the team confirms what has been done; the request does not establish a completion date or guarantee that every record can be erased.
