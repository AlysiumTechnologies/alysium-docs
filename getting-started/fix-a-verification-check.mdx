<span id="fix-a-verification-check" />

Complete or retry the browser verification check on an Alysium account form, then submit the form to continue signing in, creating an account or requesting a password reset.

This check is separate from email confirmation. If the screen asks you to open an email or enter a **Verification code**, follow that email step instead.

## Complete a waiting check

On the account form, find the verification area showing **Complete verification to continue.**

1. Complete any interaction the check requests and wait for it to succeed.
2. Select the button for the form you are completing:

| Your task | Button to select |
|---|---|
| Sign in to your account | **Sign in** |
| Register a new account | **Create an Account** |
| Request a password-reset email | **Reset Password** |

The check does not submit the form for you. Read the result after selecting the button to confirm whether your sign-in, registration or reset request succeeded. Another form attempt can ask you to complete a fresh check.

## Retry a failed check

If you see **Verification failed. Retry to continue.**, select **Retry verification**. Complete the new check on the same form and wait for it to succeed. Then select **Sign in**, **Create an Account** or **Reset Password** for your original task.

## If the submit button is still unavailable

Check the form's required fields as well as the verification:

- **Account creation:** fill in both names, a valid email and a password with at least 8 characters.
- **Sign-in:** fill in your email and password.
- **Password recovery:** fill in your email.

Correct any field message shown, then submit the form. A disabled button alone does not mean verification failed.

## If the check keeps failing

If the offered retry does not resolve the problem, email [Alysium support](mailto:support@alysium.ai). Include the form you were using, the action you tried, the exact visible message, your device and browser, and what happened after **Retry verification**. Do not include your password or an email verification code.
