<span id="log-out" />

End your session from the main app's avatar menu. Your account stays in place, and logging out does not cancel subscriptions.

If you are using a guest session, logging out ends access to its temporary chats. You can choose to stay signed in and create an account before ending that session.

<span id="open-the-avatar-menu" />

## End the session

1. On your current main app page, open or expand app navigation if it is hidden.
2. Select the avatar or user button at the bottom of the navigation.
3. Select **Log Out**.

For a named account, logout starts immediately. Wait for the sign-in screen to appear; this is the completion check for leaving the session in this browser.

## Choose at the guest warning

A guest sees **Log out of your guest session?** before the session ends. The warning explains that you will lose access to its chats.

| Choice | Result |
|---|---|
| **Stay signed in** | Closes the warning and keeps the current guest session. |
| **Log out anyway** | Ends the guest session. Wait for the sign-in screen. |

To create an account from the guest session, choose **Stay signed in**. In your current app navigation, find **Temporary Session** and select **Create Free Account**. Complete registration without logging out, then check your available conversations. You can [register with email or Google](/getting-started/create-an-alysium-account) through that flow.

## Return later or resolve a problem

To return to a named account, open [Sign in](https://app.alysium.ai/auth/login) and use its email and password or Google identity.

If logout does not reach the sign-in screen, email [Alysium support](mailto:support@alysium.ai) with the step you tried and what happened after selecting **Log Out** or **Log out anyway**.

A website widget has separate **Account** navigation. Its [visitor account options](/widget-visitors/visitor-accounts-and-saved-history) apply within that widget.
