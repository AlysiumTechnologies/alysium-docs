<span id="reset-your-password" />

Choose a new password for your existing Alysium account. You need access to that account's email inbox to open the reset link.

If you normally use Google, open [Sign in](https://app.alysium.ai/auth/login) and select **Continue with Google** with your usual Google identity first. The reset below changes your Alysium password.

## Request the reset email

1. Open [the password-reset form](https://app.alysium.ai/auth/forgot-password), or select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in form.
2. Enter your account address in **Email**. Complete the browser verification if shown, then select **Reset Password**.
3. Wait for **Check Your Email** and check the address displayed there.

This notice confirms that the request was accepted. Your password changes only after you open the email link and submit a new password.

If the browser check fails, select **Retry verification**, complete the fresh check and submit the form again.

## Open the reset link

Open the reset email and follow its password-reset link to the new-password form.

If the email is missing, check your inbox and spam folder, then check the spelling of the address shown in Alysium. If you entered the wrong address, request a reset for the correct one. If the link is invalid or expired, request a new reset link.

## Enter a new password

1. Enter a password of at least 8 characters in **New Password**, then enter the same password in **Confirm Password**.
2. Select **Change Password** and wait for the result.

If the form reports that the passwords differ or are too short, correct the fields and submit again. Typing into the fields alone does not save the password.

## Confirm access to the existing account

A successful change returns you toward sign-in with **Password updated. Sign in with your new password.** If you already have an active session, Alysium may open Home instead.

At sign-in, enter your account email and new password, complete any browser verification and select **Sign in**. Open [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces) to check your existing agents and space.

If you still cannot recover access, email [support@alysium.ai](mailto:support@alysium.ai) with the step that failed and the exact message shown. Keep your password and reset link private.
