Open [Sign in](https://app.alysium.ai/auth/login) to return to the account that holds your agents and space. Use its email and password, or the same Google identity you used before.

If a chat or purchase brought you to sign-in, finish the form already open so Alysium can return you to that task. For a current main-app guest chat, start from **Create Free Account** in the navigation's **Temporary Session** area, then select **Sign in**. This keeps the transition connected to that guest session; you can [read more about guest continuity](/getting-started/use-alysium-as-a-guest).

## Sign in with email and password

1. Enter your registered **Email** and **Password**. Use **Show password** if you need to check what you typed; **Hide password** conceals it again.
2. Complete the browser verification if shown, then select **Sign in**.
3. Wait for Alysium to open your account or the task that prompted sign-in.

If the browser check fails, select **Retry verification**, complete the fresh check and select **Sign in** again.

## Sign in with Google

1. Select **Continue with Google**.
2. Choose the Google identity you used for Alysium and complete Google's prompts.

Check the email address when choosing between Google accounts. Different identities can open different work.

## If your email needs confirmation

Check the inbox and spam folder for your account address, follow the confirmation email's instructions, then try signing in again. Email confirmation is separate from the browser verification on the form.

If you need a fresh confirmation email, complete any fresh browser check on the sign-in form, then select **Resend email** in the confirmation notice. Wait for **Sent!** and check your inbox and spam folder. If Alysium asks you to wait before another request, wait rather than repeatedly resending.

## If the password is rejected

Check both the complete email address and password. The mismatch message does not tell you which field is wrong.

For a forgotten password, select **Forgot password?** to request a reset email. You will open its reset link, enter and confirm a new password of at least 8 characters, then select **Change Password**. You can [follow the full password-recovery steps](/getting-started/reset-your-password) if needed.

## Check that you reached the right account

After successful sign-in, Alysium opens the requested destination or your current space. [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces) opens that space's agents. If expected work is missing, check which email or Google identity you used and which space is open before rebuilding anything.

For an unresolved sign-in problem, email [support@alysium.ai](mailto:support@alysium.ai) with the sign-in method, the step that failed and the exact message. Keep passwords, reset links and verification codes private.
