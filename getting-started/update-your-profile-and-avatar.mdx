<span id="update-your-profile-and-avatar" />

To request a change to your personal account name or photo, email [support@alysium.ai](mailto:support@alysium.ai). **Profile Settings** currently has no editor for those details. Include your account email and describe the name or photo change you want. Keep the reply and follow its next steps; sending a request does not confirm that a change has been made.

Your personal account, each agent and your space's creator handle identify different things. Choose the one you mean before changing a name or image.

## Which identity do you want to change?

| Identity | What it identifies | Where to change it |
|---|---|---|
| Personal account name or avatar | You, the person using Alysium. | Request a correction from the Alysium team. |
| Agent name, picture or description | One assistant that people recognize and use. | The agent's **Build** settings and **Profile** view. |
| Public creator handle | The space publishing work, including its skills. | **Profile Settings → Space Settings → Creator handle**. |

Each agent has its own profile, and each space has its own creator handle. These are separate from your account login and personal details.

<span id="find-an-agents-fields" />

## Find an agent's fields

Open [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces) and choose **Build** on the intended agent's card.

- Select **Settings** to find **Agent Name**.
- Select **Profile** to find **Agent Images** and **Description**. The avatar is the agent's profile image; the banner is its wider header image.

You can [edit the agent's name, upload its pictures and write its description](/build-your-agent/set-your-agents-name-picture-and-description) there.

## If you meant the creator handle

Open [Profile Settings](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=team), then select **Space Settings**. In **Creator handle**, enter the handle you want and check its availability. Choose an available value, select **Save handle** and wait for **Handle saved.**

The saved handle belongs to the current space. Your account login and each agent's profile are separate. You can [check the creator-handle requirements](/spaces/set-your-public-creator-handle) before choosing a new value.
