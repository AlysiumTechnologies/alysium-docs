The current **Profile Settings** screen has no personal-name or account-avatar editor. To ask about changing your personal name or photo, use [Get help from a person](/help/get-help-from-a-person) and describe the intended outcome. Sending a support request does not mean the change has been made.

Your account identity, your space's creator handle and each agent's public profile are separate. Choose the identity you mean before changing a name or image.

## Which identity do you want to change?

| Identity | What it identifies | Supported next step |
|---|---|---|
| Your personal account name or photo | You, the person using the account. | Ask the team through [Get help from a person](/help/get-help-from-a-person). |
| An agent's name, picture or description | One assistant people recognize. | Follow [Set your agent's name, picture and description](/build-your-agent/set-your-agents-name-picture-and-description). |
| Your space's public creator handle | Work published by the space, including its skills. | Follow [Set your public creator handle](/spaces/set-your-public-creator-handle). |

For a fictional workshop, **Maya** is the person using the account, the workshop handle identifies published work, and **Workshop FAQ** is an agent with its own picture. Changing one does not rename the others.

## Find an agent's fields

Agent changes require editing access to that agent. Open [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces), which opens your current space, and select the intended agent.

- **Build → Settings → Agent Name** is the assistant's name.
- **Build → Profile** contains its description and images.

Continue with [Set your agent's name, picture and description](/build-your-agent/set-your-agents-name-picture-and-description) for the actual editing steps, image inputs and save behavior. If you are already in its builder, use those same fields and continue with that guide.

Build changes an agent; it does not edit your personal photograph. Check that the resulting name or picture appears on the agent you intended.

## If you meant the creator handle

The public creator handle is a space-owner setting in **Space Settings**. [Set your public creator handle](/spaces/set-your-public-creator-handle) explains choosing an available value and saving it.

A handle change does not change the account login or replace the agent's **Agent Name** field.

For a personal account change, keep the team's reply and follow its next steps. Do not upload an account photo into an unrelated agent image control because Profile Settings has no editor.
