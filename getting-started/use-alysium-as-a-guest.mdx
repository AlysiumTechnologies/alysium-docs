Try an agent before creating a named account when that agent offers guest access. Its access limits and paid offer still apply, so a shared link does not promise unlimited free use.

## Open an available agent

1. Open the agent link shared with you, or browse [AgentHub](https://app.alysium.ai/agenthub) and choose an agent.
2. Read its description and the access or preview offer. When a chat is available, enter a question in its message box, then select **Send message** or press Enter.
3. Read the reply and any access message before continuing.

A marketplace listing may offer [a free preview](/agenthub/try-an-agent-for-free). If further use requires an account or purchase, follow the offer shown; [preview and paywall messages](/help/free-preview-and-paywall-messages) explain those choices.

## Keep a main-app guest conversation connected

To connect your guest conversation to a named account, begin from the current guest session before logging out.

1. Keep the guest page open and expand the app navigation if needed.
2. Find **Temporary Session** and select **Create Free Account**.
3. Choose how to continue:

| Your choice | What to do |
|---|---|
| Create an account with email | Enter **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email** and a **Password** of at least 8 characters. Complete the browser verification if shown, then select **Create an Account**. |
| Create an account with Google | Select **Continue with Google**, choose the Google identity you want to use and finish its prompts. |
| Use an existing account | Select **Sign in** on the account form. Enter that account's **Email** and **Password**, complete any browser verification and select **Sign in**; or use **Continue with Google** with its usual Google identity. |

Finish whichever confirmation the form presents. If **Check your email** asks for a **Verification code**, enter the emailed code and select **Verify and create account**. If **Choose a password** appears, enter a password of at least 8 characters in **Password** and select **Set password**. If you are asked to confirm an email link, follow its instructions and sign in when prompted.

If browser verification fails, select **Retry verification**, complete the new check and submit again. If the email is already registered, use **Sign in** for that existing account. Read and correct any displayed account error in the flow already open.

Wait for account setup or sign-in to finish, then check the available conversations. Starting from the current guest session helps keep them connected, but does not guarantee that every guest chat will be retained. The [account-creation guide](/getting-started/create-an-alysium-account) explains the registration choices in more detail.

## If you are using a website widget

Website widgets and phone or direct-link chats use visitor sessions separate from the main app. Do not assume a guest conversation will appear on another device, website or chat surface.

In a website widget, open **Account** for its account options. Its [visitor account and saved-history controls](/widget-visitors/visitor-accounts-and-saved-history) have their own flow.

## Choose at a guest logout warning

In the main app, open the avatar menu at the bottom of the navigation and select **Log Out**. A guest sees **Log out of your guest session?**, warning that ending the temporary session loses access to its chats.

- Select **Stay signed in** to keep the session. Use **Create Free Account** from that session if you want to connect it to a named account.
- Select **Log out anyway** to end the session and return to sign-in.

## Move from chatting to building

A guest can use the agent's offered chat, but needs a named account to build agents. After creating your account, open [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces) to find your personal space, which holds your agents, files and usage allowance. You can then [create your first agent](/build-your-agent/create-your-first-agent).
