<span id="what-a-space-is" />

A space holds the agents, Library files, plan and usage for your work. Your account identifies you when you sign in; an agent is one assistant inside a space, with its own instructions and knowledge.

When you finish setting up an Alysium account, you receive a personal space. You may own more than one space. **Workspace** is another word the app uses for a space.

## Your personal space

Spaces currently contain only their owner. Invitations and member-role changes are unavailable, so you can use your personal space without setting up a team.

You can keep several agents in one space—for example, one for customer questions and another for training materials. Creating another agent does not create another account or space. The space's plan and usage allowance support the work inside it.

Library stores reusable files in the space. Attaching a saved file as knowledge tells a particular agent to use it; saving a file in Library does not attach it to every agent. You can [use Library files in an agent](/knowledge-and-library/use-library-files-in-an-agent) when you want several assistants to draw on the same material.

## Open your current work

Open [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces) to see the agents in your active space. Choose **Build** on the intended agent's card to edit its setup.

The active space is the one Home opens and whose settings you reach through **Profile Settings**.

## Find account and space settings

Select **Profile Settings** in the sidebar or avatar menu, or [open the current space's settings](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=team). The link opens **Team**. Choose the tab for the setting you need:

| Tab | What you can find there |
|---|---|
| **Team** | Your account's displayed name and avatar. |
| **Space Settings** | The space's public creator handle. |
| **Billing** | The space's plan and subscription. |
| **Consumption** | Recorded usage. |
| **Privacy** | Your personal optional analytics choices. |
| **Creator** | Selling and creator details, visible to the space owner. |

The creator handle identifies work published by the space; an agent's name and pictures belong to that individual assistant. **Profile Settings** does not currently include a personal account-name or avatar editor.

Your [privacy choices](/getting-started/choose-your-privacy-settings) are personal, while the plan in **Billing** belongs to the space. You can [find the other main tools in Alysium](/help/where-things-are-in-alysium) as your work expands beyond Home.
