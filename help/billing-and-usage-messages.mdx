Match the notice to the affected space or agent before changing a plan or payment. A space's Pro plan and a subscription to a creator's agent are separate purchases. Running out of usage, a payment problem and unavailable subscription details need different actions.

[Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing) opens your current space's settings; check the space name there. To inspect usage, [open Profile Settings](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=team), then select **Consumption** and the relevant period.

## Subscription Details Unavailable

Alysium could not confirm the plan details. This message does not establish that the plan is Free, canceled or unpaid. Plan changes are paused while the details are unavailable.

Use **Open Billing Portal** to inspect the existing subscription, or reopen Billing to retry loading its details. Check that subscription before starting another purchase because its summary is missing.

## Included credits or an on-demand limit reached

Check the affected space's allowance and recorded usage in **Consumption**, then open **Billing**.

- **Free:** usage stops when the included allowance is spent. You can [compare the plan allowances](/plans-and-billing/free-and-pro-plans-compared) before deciding whether to upgrade.
- **Pro:** usage can stop because additional usage is disabled or the saved spending cap has been reached. Check **On-Demand Usage** beneath the plan controls.

For an active Pro subscription, the menu beside **Billing Period Limit** offers:

| Mode | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **Fixed** | Paid additional usage up to the USD amount you enter for the billing period. |
| **Unlimited** | Paid additional usage without a fixed dollar cap. |
| **Disabled** | No paid additional usage after the included credits are spent. |

Choose a mode and, for Fixed, enter a valid nonnegative dollar amount. Select **Save**, wait for the success message, then reopen Billing and check the saved mode and amount. Changing the menu or field alone does not save it. Additional usage is billed after use; lowering its limit does not erase charges already incurred.

You can [review the usage-cap choices in more detail](/plans-and-billing/turn-on-overages-and-set-a-cap). If a setting fails to save, reload Billing to check its current value, then correct any displayed field error before trying again.

## Agent bundle exhausted

In Billing, find the affected agent under **Agent Access and Subscriptions**. Its **Premium usage** meter shows the included bundle. When it is spent, the card explains: **Bundle exhausted. Additional usage depends on your workspace plan and limits.**

Check the space's plan and On-Demand Usage before expecting more usage. The agent subscription's status and any displayed bundle-reset date are separate details; a spent bundle does not itself mean the subscription was canceled or a free preview ended. You can [inspect that agent's access, payment status and usage together](/plans-and-billing/manage-your-agent-subscriptions).

## A payment needs attention

For the space's Pro subscription, use **Manage Subscription & Billing** in Billing. An individual agent's payment-warning card can offer **Fix billing** for that subscription instead.

Open the action for the subscription named in the warning, follow its payment or verification request, then return to Alysium and check the resulting status. An active Pro plan does not confirm that a separate creator-agent payment has recovered. You can [work through a failed payment](/plans-and-billing/fix-a-failed-payment) without starting another purchase to replace it.

## Creator message limit in a visitor chat

A message saying the agent has reached its monthly limit concerns the space that owns the agent. The creator needs to check that space's **Consumption** and **Billing**. A visitor cannot increase the creator's allowance from the chat.

As a visitor, buying another subscription does not increase the creator's space allowance.

## The notice still conflicts with the account status

Reopen the relevant Billing or Consumption page and compare its status with the notice. If the same problem remains, email [billing@alysium.ai](mailto:billing@alysium.ai) with the space or agent name, exact message, relevant period or payment reference, and the action you tried. Keep passwords and full card details out of the request.

A free-preview, checkout or purchased-access notice has [its own explanation and recovery](/help/free-preview-and-paywall-messages). Dismissing a notice does not complete a billing change.
