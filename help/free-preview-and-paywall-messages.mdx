A free preview, a paid agent subscription and its included usage bundle are different parts of access. Match the notice to the part that needs attention before starting a purchase or changing a payment.

For an existing purchase, open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing), which shows your current space's settings. Find **Agent Access and Subscriptions** and match the agent and creator. To inspect an offer, open [AgentHub](https://app.alysium.ai/agenthub) and choose the intended agent.

## The free preview ended

A creator can offer a limited number of free messages so you can try an agent. **Last free message**, **Free trial ended** or **Free messages used** concerns that preview, rather than a paid subscription's included bundle.

Use **See plans** or **Upgrade** when offered to inspect the subscription. In a website widget, the offer can show **Free messages used**, a monthly price, benefits and **Subscribe**. Read the recurring price, included usage and any additional-usage terms on the offer before paying. You can [review the subscription choices](/agenthub/subscribe-to-an-agent) in more detail.

If you want those terms, enter the requested payment information, select **Subscribe** and complete any payment verification. If you do not want to buy, use **Maybe Later** when shown or leave the offer. Reaching the preview limit does not itself charge you.

### Preview unavailable

**Preview unavailable** means the agent cannot currently provide a free preview because its creator's available credits are spent. It does not establish that you already used your own trial messages. Read the offer before deciding whether to subscribe; you may leave without purchasing.

## Payment is pending or checkout is incomplete

If checkout is unfinished, **Return to checkout** in a dedicated chat opens the agent's offer. Review the checkout shown, complete its requested payment or verification steps and check the result. Opening checkout, entering a card or seeing an agent in your space does not alone confirm active access.

A payment can succeed while Alysium is still confirming access. Messages such as **Payment succeeded. We are still confirming access — try again in a moment.** and **Payment succeeded, but access could not be confirmed yet.** describe that separate confirmation step. Keep the payment confirmation and recheck the matching agent's status in Billing before trying chat again. Do not start another purchase to repair an already-confirmed payment.

Once access is confirmed active, reopen the agent's chat and check whether you can send a message.

## Payment failed or access ended

For a payment warning on an agent's card in **Agent Access and Subscriptions**, use **Fix billing** when offered. It opens billing for that agent subscription. The space's top-level **Manage Subscription & Billing** action manages its separate Pro plan.

Follow the affected subscription's payment request, return to Alysium and check the resulting status. You can [resolve a failed payment](/plans-and-billing/fix-a-failed-payment) with more detail on these two billing locations.

When an agent subscription ends, existing conversations can remain read-only while new messages are unavailable. A listing becoming unavailable or an agent being disabled can also stop messaging. Read the actual notice before paying again; [read-only chat messages explain these different access states](/agenthub/why-a-marketplace-chat-is-read-only).

If the agent's Billing card offers re-subscription, that opens a new checkout at the current offer's terms. It is not an undo button for an existing scheduled cancellation.

## The included agent bundle is spent

An agent subscription can include a bundle of premium usage. In Billing, its **Premium usage** meter shows consumed and included credits. **Bundle exhausted. Additional usage depends on your workspace plan and limits.** means that bundle has been spent.

Check any bundle-reset date shown on the card. The subscription's renewal date and its bundle-reset date are separate details. If the card says **Bundle reset date unavailable**, it has not supplied a date to rely on.

Use **View usage** when offered, or [open Profile Settings](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=team) and select **Consumption**, to inspect the space's usage. Continued usage depends on the space plan and its saved limits; the agent's subscription fee does not promise unlimited chat. You can [match a space-usage notice to its next action](/help/billing-and-usage-messages).

## A confirmed purchase still does not match the notice

Recheck the affected agent and creator in Billing, then reopen the chat after access is confirmed. If the notice still conflicts with the purchase, email [billing@alysium.ai](mailto:billing@alysium.ai) with the agent and creator, exact message, payment confirmation reference and action you tried. Keep full card details out of the request.

You can [prepare a useful request to the billing team](/help/get-help-from-a-person). Brandy can explain the steps; a person handles private payment records and account decisions.
