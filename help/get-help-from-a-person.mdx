<span id="get-help-from-a-person" />

Contact the Alysium team when you need help with private account records, a billing decision or a problem that remains after the documented recovery.

## Choose the contact

| Your question | Contact |
|---|---|
| Product, account, access or data | [support@alysium.ai](mailto:support@alysium.ai) |
| Billing or a charge | [billing@alysium.ai](mailto:billing@alysium.ai) |

The email link opens your email application. If it does not open a composer, use the displayed address in your usual mail service.

Brandy can explain product steps. The team handles questions that require private account information, a payment decision or a data request.

<span id="guides-to-use-while-preparing-your-request" />

## Prepare the details

Give the team enough context to identify the task and understand what happened:

- The screen, space or agent involved.
- What you wanted to do, the action you tried and the actual result.
- The exact message displayed, if there was one.

For a display problem, include your device and browser. For billing, include the charge date, amount and confirmation reference. For an account request, writing from the account's email address helps identify it.

[Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces) opens your current space, where you can find the agent's name. [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing) shows that space's plan and separate agent subscriptions, so you can identify which purchase needs attention.

For example, an upload request could explain which document you tried to add, which agent should use it, whether the row says **Failed**, and what happened after the recovery action you actually used. Include the real message rather than guessing the cause. You can [check the available file-recovery actions](/knowledge-and-library/fix-a-file-that-failed-to-process) while gathering those details.

Keep passwords, verification codes, reset links, full card details and private document contents out of the email.

## Send the request

Write a short subject that identifies the task, such as “Document processing failed for Workshop FAQ.” Add the relevant details above and send the message to the contact you chose.

Check that your mail service shows the request as sent. Keep the team's reply and follow any verification or next-step instructions it provides. Sending the request starts the conversation; a refund, data deletion or setting change needs its own confirmation.

For access trouble, you can [match a sign-in message to its recovery](/help/sign-in-and-verification-messages). For a payment warning, [identify the affected subscription and billing portal](/plans-and-billing/fix-a-failed-payment).
