Brandy is Alysium's help agent for questions about using the product: where to find a setting, how to build an agent, how to add knowledge or what a message means. When you are talking with her, a little context helps turn a broad question into a useful next step.

You can also [build a complete practice agent from the guides](/about-alysium/how-to-get-started) at your own pace.

## Give Brandy the context for your question

Say what you want to do, which screen or section you are using and what happened. Include an agent's name or the exact message when it helps explain the problem. For example:

> I'm in Build, in the Knowledge section for my Workshop FAQ agent. The document says Processing. What does that mean, and what should I check next?

Brandy cannot see your current page, screen contents or private account records. Describe the relevant state yourself and answer a clarifying question if she asks one. Keep passwords, verification codes, reset links and full card details out of the conversation.

If you are unsure what to call a screen, you can [identify the main app, builder and visitor-chat areas](/help/where-things-are-in-alysium). For a document status, [the upload-message reference explains the stages and available recovery](/help/upload-and-file-messages).

## Turn the explanation into your next step

Brandy explains the actions; you perform them in Alysium and check the result. For an agent task, [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces) opens your current space. Choose your agent there, then open the area named in the instructions.

If a reply names a control that does not match your screen, describe the controls and message you actually see and ask for a corrected next step. After a change, use the app's saving or completion result to check what happened. A chat explanation does not confirm that a setting saved or a payment completed.

## Bring account-specific questions to a person

Use [support@alysium.ai](mailto:support@alysium.ai) for account access, data requests or an unresolved product problem. Use [billing@alysium.ai](mailto:billing@alysium.ai) for a charge or payment decision.

Brandy cannot change settings, inspect private bills, process payments or issue refunds. You can [send the team the relevant task and result](/help/get-help-from-a-person) when your question needs that help.
