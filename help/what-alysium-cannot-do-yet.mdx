These current limits help you decide whether an agent fits your task and choose an available way forward. A coming-soon label does not give a release date. If a missing capability is essential to your project, [describe that need to the Alysium team](/help/get-help-from-a-person).

## Space members and personal-account controls

A space currently contains only its owner. Invitations, member-role changes, member removal and leaving a sole-owner space are unavailable. Sharing an agent's chat link gives someone a way to use that agent; it does not add them to your space.

The sidebar and avatar menu have no space-switching picker. [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces) opens your active space, where you can find its agents.

**Profile Settings** has no editor for your personal name or account avatar. An agent has its own name, description and images in its builder. You can [find the agent profile fields](/getting-started/update-your-profile-and-avatar) if that is the identity you want to change. For an account-level data request, [contact the team with the scope of the request](/getting-started/delete-your-account-or-your-data).

## Sharing from a chat versus Deploy

The dedicated chat's **Share** window provides chat access through **Get Link**. Its editing and copy-permission choices are unavailable; its QR, email and agent-page options are incomplete.

For a standalone visitor link or QR code, open [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces), select **Build** on the intended agent's card, then select **Deploy → Share link + QR**. That separate area has the supported link and QR workflow. You can [enable the link and save its QR image](/share-and-deploy/share-your-agent-with-a-link-and-qr-code) there.

## Knowledge uploads and action tools

Library accepts supported individual files. It does not provide a complete folder-upload operation. Select the files themselves when [adding reference documents](/knowledge-and-library/upload-files-to-your-agent); Library folders can organize stored material and be attached to agents.

Paid skill and tool checkout is unavailable. Skills supply reusable guidance and reference material. You can [upload a supported skill bundle](/build-your-agent/upload-your-own-skill), but scripts inside it do not execute.

Chat tools that make changes in other apps are unavailable. Writing “make a booking” or “issue a refund” in an agent's instructions does not give it the ability to do those things. Give an information helper a real contact route for requests that need a person to act.

## Saved website knowledge versus live browsing

A website source collects readable pages and saves them as knowledge for answers. Refreshing that source can update its saved material. It does not give every chat request unrestricted live-web browsing.

For example, a shop FAQ agent can explain the return policy you supply. It cannot process a return, place an order or change a customer's records. You can [add the shop's approved website information](/knowledge-and-library/add-a-website-to-your-agents-knowledge) as a reference source.

## Help and account actions

Brandy can explain documented product steps. She cannot see your current screen or private account records, change your settings, process a payment or confirm a refund. When asking her for help, [include your goal, screen and exact message](/help/meet-brandy), then perform and check the action in the app yourself.

Account-specific access, billing and data questions need a person. Send product or account questions to [support@alysium.ai](mailto:support@alysium.ai), or charge questions to [billing@alysium.ai](mailto:billing@alysium.ai).
