Use these current limits to choose a supported way to complete your task. If a feature is essential to your project, [ask the team about that need](/help/get-help-from-a-person). A coming-soon label does not establish a release date.

## Space members and personal-account controls

A space currently contains only its owner. Inviting other members and assigning roles are unavailable. [Invite people to your space](/spaces/invite-people-to-your-space) explains the current invitation limit. Sharing an agent's chat link does not invite someone into your space.

There is no visible space-switching picker in the current sidebar or avatar menu. See [Switch spaces](/spaces/switch-spaces) for the supported entry into your current space.

**Profile Settings** has no personal-name or account-avatar editor. [Update your profile and avatar](/getting-started/update-your-profile-and-avatar) distinguishes those from an agent's name and images. For account-level requests, use [Delete your account or your data](/getting-started/delete-your-account-or-your-data).

## Sharing from a chat versus Deploy

The dedicated chat's **Share** window currently provides chat access. Its editing and copy-permission choices are unavailable, and its QR, email and agent-page forms are incomplete.

The separate **Deploy** area has a working link and QR flow. Use [Share your agent with a link and QR code](/share-and-deploy/share-your-agent-with-a-link-and-qr-code) for that task. The limits in the chat's Share window do not prevent this separate sharing method.

## Knowledge uploads and action tools

Library accepts supported individual files. It does not provide a complete folder-upload operation. Use [Upload files to your agent](/knowledge-and-library/upload-files-to-your-agent) for reference documents.

Paid skill and tool checkout is unavailable. Attached skills provide guidance and references; uploaded scripts do not execute. [Upload your own skill](/build-your-agent/upload-your-own-skill) explains the supported bundle path.

Attached chat tools that perform actions in other apps are not available in this version. Instructions describing an action do not give an agent the ability to perform it.

## Saved website knowledge versus live browsing

[Adding a website to your agent's knowledge](/knowledge-and-library/add-a-website-to-your-agents-knowledge) collects readable website content for answers. It does not give the agent unrestricted live-web browsing during every conversation or the ability to place an order or change another service's records.

For example, a fictional shop FAQ agent can explain approved return notes. It should not claim that it processed a return.

## Help and account actions

Brandy cannot infer your current screen or account, or perform the account changes she describes. Use the guides to act in the app, and use [Get help from a person](/help/get-help-from-a-person) for private records or unavailable operations. [Meet Brandy](/help/meet-brandy) explains how to give her useful context.
