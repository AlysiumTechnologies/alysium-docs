<span id="cancel-pro" />

Cancel the Pro plan for your Alysium space through its existing billing portal. Review when the cancellation takes effect, then check the result in Alysium.

Pro applies to the space that holds your agents, files and credit allowance. Subscriptions to individual creators' agents have separate bills. If you want to stop one of those instead, you can [cancel that agent's subscription](/agenthub/cancel-an-agent-subscription).

## Open the existing Pro plan

1. Open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing). Check the space name above the settings content.
2. Wait for the details to load, then read the plan and status under **Billing & Subscription**.
3. Select **Manage Subscription & Billing** to open the existing plan's portal.

If Billing already shows **Cancels on** with the date you intended, the cancellation is scheduled. The subscription can still show an active status until that date.

For an existing plan that is no longer active, **View Billing & Invoices** opens its existing billing record. If you see **Subscription Details Unavailable**, Alysium could not confirm the plan details; reopen Billing or select **Open Billing Portal** when offered.

## Cancel in the portal

1. Check that the portal shows the intended Pro subscription, then open its offered cancellation option.
2. Read the effective date, remaining access and cancellation terms. Confirm only if they match what you want.
3. Keep the confirmation, then return to Alysium and reopen [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing).
4. Check the resulting subscription status and any **Cancels on** date against the confirmation.

Use the confirmed date to understand how long the plan remains in effect. When the plan changes, you can [check its allowance and recorded usage](/plans-and-billing/check-your-usage).

If you also intend to stop renewals for purchased agents, scroll to **Agent Access and Subscriptions** in Billing and [review those subscriptions separately](/plans-and-billing/manage-your-agent-subscriptions).

## If cancellation is missing or unclear

If the portal does not offer cancellation, or its confirmed result still does not match Billing after reopening it, contact [billing@alysium.ai](mailto:billing@alysium.ai). Include the space and plan name, displayed status and date, intended change and any confirmation reference.

If the portal fails to open, read the error shown in Billing and retry from the existing plan's control. Starting another subscription does not complete a cancellation. You can also use the existing portal to [check invoices or payment details](/plans-and-billing/update-your-payment-method-or-see-invoices).
