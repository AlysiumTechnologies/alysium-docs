<span id="check-your-usage" />

Use **Consumption** to see your space's credit allowance, recorded usage and remaining included credits. For a purchased agent's own included bundle, check its separate meter in Billing.

## Open Consumption

1. Open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing), which opens the current space's settings. Check the space name above the settings content.
2. Select the **Consumption** tab. The page heading is **Usage & Consumption**.

## Read the space balance

Check the period displayed between the **«** and **»** controls, then select **Summary**. Wait for the usage amounts to load and compare:

- **Current Usage**: credits used against the space's included allowance for that period.
- The percentage used and the remaining included credits beneath the meter.

Use **«** to examine an earlier period and **»** to return toward the current one. The forward control becomes unavailable at the current period. Return there before deciding how much usage is available now.

Choose another view when you want to understand the activity behind the balance:

| View | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Summary** | The space's used credits, included allowance and remaining amount for the selected period. |
| **Detailed View** | Usage by AI model, with input and output tokens, credit rates and credits used where available. Tokens are pieces of text the model processes. |
| **By Source** | Recorded usage grouped under **Your platform plan** and purchased agents, with model, token and credit totals. |

You can switch views and periods without saving. **By Source** helps identify the source of activity; use an agent's own meter to check its remaining bundle.

A zero remaining included balance does not always stop Pro usage. If Pro is active and paid on-demand usage is enabled, usage can continue within the space's saved additional-spending limit. You can [review that setting in Billing](/plans-and-billing/turn-on-overages-and-set-a-cap).

<span id="check-a-purchased-agents-bundle" />

## Check a purchased agent's bundle

1. Open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing) and scroll to **Agent Access and Subscriptions**.
2. Find the card by both agent and creator name.
3. If the card shows **Premium usage**, compare the consumed credits with its bundle limit. Read the reset or access information beneath the meter.

An active bundle can show **Bundle reset scheduled for** a date or **Bundle reset date unavailable**. An ending subscription or a payment issue can instead show how the remaining usage depends on access. Use the information on that card rather than calculating a reset from the subscription's renewal date.

The bundle pays for that agent's usage first. Once it is spent, further usage depends on your space's allowance and limits. The agent's subscription renewal date concerns its bill; the bundle reset concerns included usage. [How credits work](/plans-and-billing/how-credits-work) explains these separate allowances.

The card's **View usage** action, when shown, opens the space's general Consumption view.

## If the amounts look unexpected

Check the space and selected period before interpreting an empty view or an unexpected amount. Reopen Consumption to check again. If the balance still conflicts with a usage notice, contact [billing@alysium.ai](mailto:billing@alysium.ai) with the space name, displayed period, amounts and exact notice.

For a payment, access or renewal issue on an agent card, [inspect that subscription's status](/plans-and-billing/manage-your-agent-subscriptions). Those states are separate from the credit balance.
