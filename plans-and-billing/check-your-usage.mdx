Use **Consumption** to see the current space's credit allowance, how much was used and where that usage was recorded. A purchased agent's included bundle has its own meter in Billing.

## Open Consumption

The [settings link](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=team) opens the current space's **Profile Settings**. It initially selects **Team**; choose **Consumption** for usage.

Already in Profile Settings for the intended space? Select **Consumption**, then continue with [Read the space balance](#read-the-space-balance). Already on **Usage & Consumption**? Continue there directly.

1. Open [Profile Settings](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=team).
2. Check that you are viewing the intended space.
3. Select **Consumption**.
4. Continue with [Read the space balance](#read-the-space-balance).

## Read the space balance

1. Check the period displayed between the previous and next arrow controls.
2. Select **Summary**.
3. Compare the used-credit amount, allowance and remaining amount.

Use the period arrows to examine an earlier period. Return to the current period before deciding how much usage is available now; a historical period is not today's balance. Do not add unrelated totals from different periods together.

Select the view that answers your question:

| View | What it helps you understand |
|---|---|
| **Summary** | The space's plan-level usage meter and remaining allowance for the selected period. |
| **Detailed View** | Usage by AI model, with recorded input, output and credit information where available. |
| **By Source** | Attribution to a purchased agent's included bundle or the platform plan. |

These views show recorded usage. There is no **Save** action, and selecting a tab does not change your spending limit. Once you have the correct space, period and view, you can use those amounts to understand the balance or compare activity.

## Check a purchased agent's bundle

If an agent's included bundle appears spent while the space still has credits, use **By Source** to understand the recorded attribution. Then check that agent's separate allowance:

1. Open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing).
2. Find **Agent Access and Subscriptions**.
3. Locate the card by both agent and creator name.
4. Read its premium-usage meter and bundle reset information, where shown.

The agent's bundle and your space allowance are separate. Its subscription renewal date is also separate from the bundle reset date. [How credits work](/plans-and-billing/how-credits-work) explains which allowance is used first.

## If the amounts look unexpected

An empty period can mean no usage was recorded for that period. Check the space and period before assuming data is missing. If the current balance still conflicts with a usage warning after reloading the page, contact billing@alysium.ai with the space, period and exact warning.

To change additional spending, use [Turn on overages and set a cap](/plans-and-billing/turn-on-overages-and-set-a-cap). Consumption only shows usage. For an agent's access, renewal or payment state, use [Manage your agent subscriptions](/plans-and-billing/manage-your-agent-subscriptions).
