<span id="fix-a-failed-payment" />

Resolve the payment issue on the affected subscription, then check its status in Alysium. Saving a replacement card is one step; also confirm the result of any unpaid invoice or payment action.

Read whether the warning concerns your space's **Alysium Pro** plan or a **creator's agent**. They have separate bills and payment-management paths. An active Pro plan does not settle an agent's unpaid bill, and an agent payment does not settle Pro.

## Resolve a Pro payment problem

1. Open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing) and check the space name above the settings content.
2. Wait for the subscription details to load, then read the plan and status under **Billing & Subscription**.
3. Select **Manage Subscription & Billing**. For an existing plan that is no longer active, use **View Billing & Invoices**. If the page says **Subscription Details Unavailable**, use **Open Billing Portal** when offered.
4. In the portal, confirm the intended Pro subscription and open the payment issue or unpaid invoice shown there.
5. Update the payment method if needed, save or confirm the change, and complete any payment or verification action the portal offers. Check its payment result and keep the confirmation.
6. Return to Alysium and reopen [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing). Check the resulting Pro status.

A Pro payment issue can pause space-funded usage even when credits remain. Changing an on-demand limit does not resolve an unpaid bill.

## Find the affected agent

1. Open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing) for the space that obtained the agent.
2. Scroll to **Agent Access and Subscriptions** and find the card by both agent and creator name.
3. Select **Fix billing** when the card shows a payment warning. It opens billing for that creator's subscription.

## Check the creator-agent payment in its portal

1. Confirm that the portal shows the affected creator's subscription, then open its payment issue or unpaid invoice.
2. Update the payment details if needed and confirm the change. Complete any payment or verification action offered, then check the result and keep its confirmation.
3. Return to Alysium and reopen [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing). Check the named agent's card, then reopen the affected chat to inspect its access notice.

An agent payment warning may leave access temporarily available while payment is retried. If chat is paused because of payment, remaining bundle credits do not remove that block. The space's Pro plan has separate payment checks.

## If the warning remains

If the portal fails to open, read the error shown in Billing and retry from the existing record. If it offers no way to resolve the issue, or a confirmed payment still does not match Alysium's status after reopening Billing, contact [billing@alysium.ai](mailto:billing@alysium.ai). Include the space or agent and creator names, displayed status and payment confirmation reference. Do not send full card details.

Keep the existing subscription record while the issue is checked; another checkout can create a separate purchase. If the notice concerns ended access or pending checkout instead of a failed payment, [check what is making the marketplace chat read-only](/agenthub/why-a-marketplace-chat-is-read-only). For routine card changes or invoice history, you can [manage payment details without a warning](/plans-and-billing/update-your-payment-method-or-see-invoices).
