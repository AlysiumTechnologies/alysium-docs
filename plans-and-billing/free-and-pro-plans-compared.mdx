Free and Pro apply to an Alysium **space**: the area that holds your agents, files and usage allowance. Choose a plan by comparing the included credits, model choices and storage you need, then decide whether to allow paid AI usage beyond the included allowance.

## Compare the allowances

| | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Plan fee | Free plan. | A recurring subscription. Review the price and billing interval in checkout. |
| Included AI usage | The space's included credit allowance, shown in Consumption. | The included credits shown in the Pro offer; Consumption shows your space's actual allowance. |
| After included credits are spent | Usage stops at the allowance. | Usage stops unless paid on-demand usage is enabled. A saved fixed cap can also stop further usage. |
| AI models | Models available on Free. | Also unlocks model choices that require a subscription. |
| Library storage | The Free storage allowance. | A larger storage allowance. |

Credits measure AI usage, rather than a fixed number of replies. The model and amount of information it processes affect how many credits a request uses. You can [see how the allowances are used](/plans-and-billing/how-credits-work) before estimating your needs.

**On-demand usage** is paid AI usage after the space's included credits are spent. Active Pro can use **Disabled** to stop at the included allowance, **Fixed** to allow additional usage within a USD period limit, or **Unlimited** to remove that fixed additional-spending cap. Unlimited does not mean free additional usage. You can [choose and save an on-demand limit](/plans-and-billing/turn-on-overages-and-set-a-cap) separately from the recurring plan fee.

Library capacity is a separate allowance from AI credits. Check [storage limits](/knowledge-and-library/storage-limits) when comparing how much reference material you need to keep.

## A space plan is separate from an agent subscription

A creator's paid agent has its own offer, subscription fee and possible included usage bundle. Buying that agent does not upgrade your space to Pro, and Pro does not buy access to every paid agent.

You can [inspect those agent purchases](/plans-and-billing/manage-your-agent-subscriptions) separately, including their prices, renewal states and included usage.

## Find your current plan and usage

1. Open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing), which opens the current space's settings. Check the space name above the settings content.
2. Wait for the plan details to load in **Billing & Subscription**. Read the plan, subscription status and any renewal or cancellation date shown.
3. Select the **Consumption** settings tab. In **Summary**, check the displayed period, used credits, allowance and remaining amount. Use the current period when deciding how much included usage is available now.

The other Consumption views help you [inspect model usage and recorded sources](/plans-and-billing/check-your-usage).

If Billing shows **Subscription Details Unavailable**, reopen Billing or use **Open Billing Portal** when offered to inspect the existing plan. A failed details request does not establish that you need another subscription.

## Decide whether to upgrade

Free can be a useful starting point while you build an agent and watch its usage. Compare Pro's displayed offer if you need subscription-required models, more included usage or larger Library capacity. Decide separately whether you want additional charges after the included credits are spent.

When you choose to [upgrade the space to Pro](/plans-and-billing/upgrade-to-pro), review the actual recurring price, included credits, billing interval and payment terms before confirming checkout.
