A credit measures Alysium AI usage. One credit represents **\$0.01 of usage**, rather than a fixed number of messages.

Your space has a plan allowance. A purchased agent can also have its own included bundle, so the balance that matters depends on which agent you are using.

## Why messages use different amounts

The model producing a reply and the amount of information it processes affect the credits used. A short question and a request involving lengthy reference material can cost different amounts. An allowance of 100 credits therefore does not promise 100 replies.

**Tokens** are pieces of text processed by a model. **Credits** measure the resulting usage charged to an allowance. Consumption can show both: a token count helps explain how much text was processed, while a credit amount tells you the usage recorded.

## Which allowance pays for an interaction

For an agent funded by your space, usage draws on the space's plan allowance. A purchased agent with an included bundle uses that bundle first. After the bundle is spent, further usage depends on the space's plan and limits.

For example, suppose a fictional agent offer includes 100 credits. Those credits cover usage for that agent. Once they are spent, the chat may still continue using the space's available allowance. If the space has also spent its included credits, active Pro needs paid on-demand usage enabled and room within any saved cap to continue. Free pauses at its allowance.

The agent's recurring fee pays for that agent subscription; it does not upgrade the space to Pro. The space's Pro subscription and its on-demand settings are separate. Buying the same agent again is not a way to reset its bundle.

## Where to see the recorded amounts

Open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing) and check the space name above settings. Select **Consumption** to open **Usage & Consumption**.

Check the displayed period, then choose the view that answers your question:

| View | What to read |
|---|---|
| **Summary** | Used credits, the space's included allowance and the remaining amount. |
| **Detailed View** | Model usage, with input/output token and credit details where available. |
| **By Source** | Recorded usage grouped under purchased agents and **Your platform plan**. |

By Source helps identify the source associated with usage. To check an agent's remaining bundle, return to **Billing**, scroll to **Agent Access and Subscriptions**, and find its card by both agent and creator name. Read **Premium usage** and the bundle reset information where shown. **View usage** opens the space's general Consumption view.

A zero remaining amount in Summary can mean the included space allowance is spent while paid on-demand usage is still available. Check the saved **On-Demand Usage** setting in Billing to understand additional spending. You can [inspect the usage views and period controls](/plans-and-billing/check-your-usage) in more detail.

## Resets, renewals and payment warnings

Use Consumption's displayed period when reading the space allowance. An agent card's **Renews** date concerns its recurring bill; **Bundle reset scheduled for** concerns its included usage. Those dates can differ. If the card says the reset date is unavailable, use that state rather than calculating a date from the last message or renewal.

Spending a bundle does not cancel the agent subscription. Remaining credits also do not settle an unpaid bill or prove that access is active. A payment warning may allow agent access temporarily while payment is retried, or the affected chat may be paused.

For an allowance limit, [check the space balance and agent bundle](/plans-and-billing/what-happens-when-you-run-out-of-credits). For a payment warning, [resolve the affected subscription's payment](/plans-and-billing/fix-a-failed-payment). You can also [inspect an agent's access and renewal state](/plans-and-billing/manage-your-agent-subscriptions) without changing its subscription.
