Choose whether your Pro space allows paid AI usage after its included credits are spent, and set a limit for that additional spending. You manage this setting for the space you own. Paid on-demand usage requires active Pro.

## Choose what additional spending is allowed

**On-Demand Usage** is additional usage billed after use. The **Billing Period Limit** applies to this spending; the recurring Pro fee is separate.

| Mode | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **Fixed** | Additional usage up to a USD limit for the billing period. |
| **Unlimited** | Paid additional usage without a fixed dollar cap. |
| **Disabled** | Usage stops when the included credits are spent. |

For example, **Fixed** with an amount of **10** sets a \$10 additional-usage limit for the billing period, on top of the Pro fee. A \$0 limit still allows the included credits but no paid additional usage.

The **Usage** meter shows on-demand spending accumulated in the current billing period. In **Fixed** mode, it shows that spending against the limit. Lowering the limit or choosing **Disabled** does not erase usage already recorded. Usage already in progress may bring recorded spending above a fixed cap.

This setting applies to the space's platform usage. It does not buy access to a creator's agent or add credits to that agent's included bundle.

## Set and save the limit

1. Open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing) and check the space name at the top, above the settings content.
2. Scroll below the plan controls to **Overage Billing** and its **On-Demand Usage** section.
3. Open the menu beside **Billing Period Limit** and choose **Fixed**, **Unlimited** or **Disabled**.
4. For **Fixed**, enter your limit in the adjacent USD amount field. Use a valid nonnegative dollar amount.
5. Select **Save**. Wait for **Updating your plan...** to finish and check the success message confirming that overage billing was enabled or disabled.
6. Reopen [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing) and scroll back to **On-Demand Usage**. Check the saved mode and, for **Fixed**, the amount.

Choosing a mode or editing the amount alone does not save it. You can also [check Consumption](/plans-and-billing/check-your-usage) to see the space's allowance and recorded usage.

<span id="if-the-controls-or-save-are-unavailable" />

## If the limit does not save

For **Fixed**, the amount field may show **Enter an amount**, **Enter a valid number** or **Must be at least \$0**. Correct the amount to enable **Save**.

If Billing shows a save error, reload Billing and inspect the saved value before trying again. If you cannot confirm it, contact [billing@alysium.ai](mailto:billing@alysium.ai) with the space name, intended mode and amount, and the displayed error.

If the subscription has a payment problem, use **Manage Subscription & Billing** for the existing plan to inspect it. A successful limit save does not settle a failed payment. You can [check the payment result](/plans-and-billing/fix-a-failed-payment) separately.
