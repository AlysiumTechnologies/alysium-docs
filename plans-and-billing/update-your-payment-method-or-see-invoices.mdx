<span id="update-your-payment-method-or-see-invoices" />

Use the billing portal for the subscription you want to manage. Your space's Pro plan and a subscription to a creator's agent are separate bills, with separate portals.

Check the subscription name on the charge or confirmation. A charge for a named agent belongs to that creator's subscription, even if its amount resembles your Pro payment.

<span id="open-the-existing-pro-plans-portal" />

## Open the existing Pro plan's portal

1. Open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing) and check the space name at the top, above the settings content.
2. Wait for the subscription details to load under **Billing & Subscription**.
3. Select the portal button offered for the existing plan:

   | Button | Where it appears |
   |---|---|
   | **Manage Subscription & Billing** | Under **Manage Your Plan** for an existing subscription. |
   | **View Billing & Invoices** | Alongside the options for a canceled plan. |
   | **Open Billing Portal** | Under **Subscription Details Unavailable** when existing subscription details could not load. |

4. Check that the portal shows the intended Pro subscription.

The portal's available payment and invoice options depend on its configuration. Use the options it actually offers.

## Update a payment method

1. Open the payment-method option offered in the subscription's portal.
2. Enter the replacement or updated details requested there and complete its confirmation.
3. Check the result and the saved payment details shown in the portal.
4. Return to Alysium and reopen [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing). Check the subscription's resulting status.

If you are resolving an unpaid invoice, check whether that invoice has been paid as well. Saving a card alone does not confirm payment. You can [check and resolve a failed payment](/plans-and-billing/fix-a-failed-payment) for the affected subscription.

## Inspect an invoice

1. Open the billing or invoice history offered in the subscription's portal.
2. Select the relevant bill and compare its subscription, date and amount with the charge you are investigating.
3. If the portal offers a download and you use it, open the file and check that it is the intended bill.

Matching those details identifies the invoice for that charge.

<span id="open-payment-management-for-a-creators-agent" />

## Open payment management for a creator's agent

1. Open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing) and scroll to **Agent Access and Subscriptions**.
2. Match both the agent name and creator to the subscription you want to manage.
3. Select **Fix billing** if the agent's card offers a payment warning with that action.
4. Check that the creator's portal shows the intended agent subscription. Use its offered payment-method option to enter and confirm updated details, or open its billing history to find an invoice. Check the saved payment result or the invoice's subscription, date and amount.
5. After changing payment details, reopen [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing) and inspect that agent's status.

**Fix billing** appears for payment warnings; it is not offered on every active agent card. If the card has no billing action and you need a card change or invoice, contact [billing@alysium.ai](mailto:billing@alysium.ai) with the agent and creator names and your purchase reference. Changes in the Pro portal apply to the space's plan, so use the creator's portal for this agent bill.

## If the portal will not open or the result is unclear

If Billing shows an error opening a portal, read it and retry from the existing subscription's record. Reopen Billing to retry a subscription-details loading error. Use the existing bill to find its invoice rather than starting another subscription.

If the problem remains, contact [billing@alysium.ai](mailto:billing@alysium.ai) with the space or agent name, the displayed status and an invoice or confirmation reference. Keep full card details in the payment portal.
