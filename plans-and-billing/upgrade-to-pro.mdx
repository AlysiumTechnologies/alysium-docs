Upgrade the Alysium space you own to Pro and choose whether to allow paid usage beyond its included credits.

A space holds your agents, files and usage allowance. Pro applies to that space; subscriptions to individual creators' agents are separate purchases. You can [compare Free and Pro](/plans-and-billing/free-and-pro-plans-compared) to see their included allowances and plan differences.

## Check the space and existing plan

1. Open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing), which opens the current space's settings. Check the space name at the top, above the settings content.
2. Wait for the subscription details to finish loading, then check the plan and status under **Billing & Subscription**.

If you see **Manage Your Plan**, use **Manage Subscription & Billing** for that existing subscription. You can [update its payment method or see invoices](/plans-and-billing/update-your-payment-method-or-see-invoices) there.

If you see **Subscription Details Unavailable**, Alysium could not load the existing subscription's details. Reopen Billing to try loading them again, or select **Open Billing Portal** to inspect that subscription.

If Billing shows **Resubscribe to Pro**, select **Resubscribe Now** with its displayed monthly price if you want to restart the plan. This path uses your saved overage preference and does not show the switch for a new subscription. Check **On-Demand Usage** after checkout.

## Choose overages for a new subscription

For a new subscription, **Enable automatic overages** controls whether ordinary platform usage can continue after the included credits run out:

- Turn it off if you want usage to stop when the included credits are spent.
- Turn it on if you want to allow paid additional usage, billed after use and separately from the recurring Pro fee.

Select **Subscribe Now** with its displayed monthly price to open checkout. The button may also say **with Overages** when overages are enabled.

## Review checkout and check the plan

1. Review the plan name, recurring price, billing interval and payment terms in checkout. Use the price shown there before paying.
2. Enter the requested payment information. Confirm the purchase if the charge and terms match what you want, and complete any payment verification requests.
3. Check checkout's result, then return to Alysium.
4. Reopen [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing) and check the resulting plan and subscription status. An active Pro status confirms the upgrade; opening checkout or entering a card does not.

Once Pro is active, scroll below the plan controls to **Overage Billing** and its **On-Demand Usage** section. Check the saved choice for additional spending after either a new subscription or a resubscription.

To change that choice, open the menu beside **Billing Period Limit**:

| Setting | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **Fixed** | Additional usage up to a USD limit for the billing period. Enter a valid nonnegative dollar amount in the adjacent field. A \$0 limit allows included credits but no paid additional usage. |
| **Unlimited** | Paid additional usage without a fixed dollar cap. |
| **Disabled** | Usage stops when the included credits are spent. |

Select **Save**, wait for **Updating your plan...** to finish and check the success message. Reopen Billing and check that **On-Demand Usage** shows the saved mode and, for **Fixed**, the amount you entered. Choosing a mode or editing the amount alone does not save it.

Lowering [the additional-usage limit](/plans-and-billing/turn-on-overages-and-set-a-cap) does not erase charges already incurred. Usage already in progress may bring recorded spending above a fixed cap. You can [check Consumption](/plans-and-billing/check-your-usage) for the allowance and recorded usage.

## If the upgrade does not finish

If Billing or checkout shows an error, use the displayed message and check Billing before trying another purchase. For a payment problem on an existing subscription, use **Manage Subscription & Billing** to inspect its payment status. You can [check and resolve a failed payment](/plans-and-billing/fix-a-failed-payment) there.

If the plan still appears unchanged after payment confirmation, keep that confirmation. Contact [billing@alysium.ai](mailto:billing@alysium.ai) with the space name, displayed status and confirmation reference. Starting another checkout will not verify the first payment.

If an on-demand setting fails to save, reload Billing and check the saved value before trying again. Correct any field validation message for **Fixed**. If you cannot confirm the setting, contact billing@alysium.ai with the space name, intended mode and amount, and the error. Saving an overage setting does not resolve a failed payment.
