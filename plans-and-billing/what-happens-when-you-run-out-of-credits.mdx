<span id="what-happens-when-you-run-out-of-credits" />

Chat can pause when an included allowance is spent or a saved additional-spending cap is reached. Check which allowance is involved before changing a plan: your space's credits, a purchased agent's bundle and a free preview each have their own limits.

## Match the notice to the next check

| What the notice concerns | What to inspect |
|---|---|
| The space's allowance or additional-spending limit | The current period in **Consumption**, then the plan and saved **On-Demand Usage** setting in Billing. |
| A purchased agent's included usage | That agent and creator's card under **Agent Access and Subscriptions** in Billing. |
| A failed payment or paused subscription | The affected subscription's payment status. Remaining credits do not resolve a payment problem. |
| The end of a free preview | The agent's paid offer, if you want to keep using it, or the option to stop. This preview is separate from your space's credits. |

A general **Usage limit reached** message may not identify the cause on its own. Compare it with the actual balance and subscription status. For a payment warning, [resolve the existing bill](/plans-and-billing/fix-a-failed-payment). If a free preview has ended, you can [review the choice to subscribe](/agenthub/try-an-agent-for-free) before deciding whether to pay.

## Check the space balance and cap

1. Open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing). Check the space name above the settings content, then read its plan and subscription status.
2. Select **Consumption**. On **Usage & Consumption**, check the period between the **«** and **»** controls and select **Summary**. If you are viewing an earlier period, use **»** to return to the current one.
3. Wait for the amounts to load, then compare used credits with the included allowance and remaining amount.
4. Select **Billing** again. For Pro, scroll below the plan controls to **Overage Billing** and **On-Demand Usage**, where shown. Compare the saved **Billing Period Limit** with the **Usage** amount.

| Plan or setting | After the included credits are spent |
|---|---|
| Free | Usage stops at the allowance. |
| Active Pro with **Disabled** on-demand usage | Usage stops at the allowance. |
| Active Pro with on-demand usage enabled | Paid usage can continue within the saved limit. A reached **Fixed** cap can stop it. |

You can wait for the next allowance period, using the dates shown in Consumption, then check the new balance. If you need more usage sooner on Free, you can [review Pro and its checkout terms](/plans-and-billing/upgrade-to-pro). Pro is a recurring subscription; additional usage is a separate spending choice.

For active Pro, decide whether you want to permit more paid usage before changing **Billing Period Limit**:

| Setting | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **Fixed** | Paid additional usage up to a USD limit for the billing period. Enter a valid nonnegative amount in the adjacent USD field. |
| **Unlimited** | Paid additional usage without a fixed dollar cap. |
| **Disabled** | No additional usage after the included credits are spent. |

1. Open the menu beside **Billing Period Limit** and choose a mode. For **Fixed**, enter the amount you intend to allow.
2. Select **Save**. Wait for **Updating your plan...** to finish and for the success message.
3. Reopen [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing), scroll to **On-Demand Usage** and check the saved mode and, for **Fixed**, the amount.

Editing the mode or amount alone does not save it. A zero fixed limit permits no additional usage. Lowering or disabling a limit does not erase charges already incurred. Usage already in progress can take spending beyond a fixed amount; the cap is checked against recorded usage before new work starts. You can [read more about setting a cap](/plans-and-billing/turn-on-overages-and-set-a-cap) before choosing one.

If a **Fixed** amount has a validation message, correct it before saving. If saving fails, reopen Billing and inspect the saved value before trying again. After a saved change or a new allowance period, check the balance and setting, then return to chat.

## Check the agent's bundle

1. Open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing) for the space that obtained the agent and scroll to **Agent Access and Subscriptions**.
2. Match both the agent and creator name. Read the subscription's status and, where shown, the **Premium usage** meter.
3. Compare consumed credits with the bundle limit and read the reset or access information beneath the meter.

The agent's included bundle is used first. After it is spent, continued usage depends on your space's allowance and limits. Spending the bundle does not cancel the agent's subscription, and buying the agent again is not a bundle-reset action.

Use the card's actual bundle-reset information. Its subscription renewal date concerns billing and can differ from the bundle reset; an unavailable reset date cannot be worked out from the renewal date. [How credits work](/plans-and-billing/how-credits-work) explains the two allowances.

## If you are a website visitor

When the notice concerns your own purchased agent, open [Billing](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=billing) and find its agent and creator under **Agent Access and Subscriptions**. Check the payment status and bundle there.

If the notice concerns the site or agent owner's allowance, share the exact message with that owner. They need to check the space that owns the agent. A visitor cannot raise that allowance from the chat, and buying a visitor subscription may not resolve that pause.

## If the warning conflicts with the balance

Reopen Consumption for the intended space and current period, then check the plan status, the agent's card and any saved cap in Billing. If the notice still conflicts with those amounts or a confirmed setting change, contact [billing@alysium.ai](mailto:billing@alysium.ai) with the space and agent names, displayed amounts, status and exact notice.

If the notice instead says access ended, payment is pending or the listing is unavailable, [check the agent's access state](/agenthub/why-a-marketplace-chat-is-read-only) before changing a spending limit.
