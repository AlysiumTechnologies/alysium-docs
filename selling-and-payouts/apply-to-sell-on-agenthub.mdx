Submit a creator application for permission to list in the curated AgentHub storefront. Selling through your own widget or direct chat link has separate readiness requirements and does not require this storefront approval.

Application approval does not approve every agent listing or complete payment setup. Use your named Alysium account. Have your full legal name and a short explanation of your expertise and planned agents ready; a portfolio and sample agents are optional.

**Already in the application?** Continue with [Fill the application](#fill-the-application). To inspect an earlier submission, use [Check your review status](#check-your-review-status).

## Open the application

1. Open [AgentHub](https://app.alysium.ai/agenthub).
2. Select **Share your agent**.
3. Continue with [Fill the application](#fill-the-application), or [Check your review status](#check-your-review-status) if an application already exists.

The AgentHub link opens the general storefront. If it asks you to sign in, use your named account and open AgentHub again if needed.

## Fill the application

**Marketplace Display Name** shows the space name and is read-only in this form. Use your own full name in **Legal Name**.

For **Why do you want to be a creator?**, explain your expertise, intended users and the help your agents will offer. For example:

> I help independent café owners write clear customer information. I plan to build agents that answer questions from each café's approved FAQ and direct unresolved questions to staff.

This is an illustrative statement. Replace it with your actual experience and plans; it supplies neither an identity nor credentials.

1. Review **Marketplace Display Name**.
2. Enter your full name in **Legal Name**.
3. Complete **Why do you want to be a creator?**.
4. Add published work in **Portfolio/Website** if you want to include it.
5. Select your own existing agents under **Sample Agents (Optional)** if you want to include them.
6. Select **Submit Application**.
7. Wait for **Application Submitted!**, or read any validation/error message.

A lack of sample agents is not a reason to invent one.

## Check your review status

Reopen **Share your agent** in AgentHub to view the existing application and its status.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending | A decision has not yet been made. |
| Approved | You can proceed with storefront listing work. |
| Rejected | The application was not approved at that time. Read any supplied review note. |

Do not submit repeated applications while a pending record exists. Once approved, continue with [List your agent on Agenthub](/selling-and-payouts/list-your-agent-on-agenthub). Use [Set up Stripe to get paid](/selling-and-payouts/set-up-stripe-to-get-paid) separately for payment onboarding.

## If the form will not submit

**Legal Name** and the expertise explanation must be filled. Correct the field message before submitting again. After an uncertain result, reopen Share your agent and check the existing application before retrying.
