Apply for permission to submit agents to the curated AgentHub storefront. Your application describes your expertise and the agents you want to offer. Selling through your own widget or standalone chat link has separate setup requirements and does not need this storefront approval.

## Open the application

Open [AgentHub](https://app.alysium.ai/agenthub) and select **Share your agent** near the top of the page. A new application opens **Become an Alysium Creator**. If you have already applied, the same control opens your application status instead.

## Fill the application

**Marketplace Display Name** shows your current space's name and cannot be edited in this form.

1. Enter your full legal name in **Legal Name**.
2. In **Why do you want to be a creator?**, explain your expertise, whom your agents will help and what they will do. For example: “I help independent café owners write customer information. My agents will answer visitors' questions from each café's approved FAQ and direct unresolved questions to staff.”
3. Add a valid link in **Portfolio/Website** if you want reviewers to see your work.
4. Under **Sample Agents (Optional)**, select any of your existing agents you want to include. You can apply without samples; they do not need to be published.
5. Select **Submit Application** and wait for **Application Submitted!**. The dialog then closes automatically.

Submission sends the application for review. It does not approve an individual listing or complete payment setup.

## Check your review status

Reopen **Share your agent** in AgentHub to read **Creator Application Status**.

| Status | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Pending Review** | Check back here for the decision. Your submitted application is shown instead of a new form. |
| **✓ Approved** | You can [prepare and submit an agent listing](/selling-and-payouts/list-your-agent-on-agenthub). Each listing has its own review. |
| **Application Rejected** | Read the **Reason**, if supplied. Contact [support@alysium.ai](mailto:support@alysium.ai) about reapplying. |

The status view includes the submission date and, after review, any supplied review date or reason. Use this view to check the decision rather than relying on an email.

## If the form will not submit

Fill **Legal Name** and **Why do you want to be a creator?**, and correct any field message. If you supplied a website, use a valid full URL.

If a submission reports an error or its result is uncertain, reopen **Share your agent** and check whether the application exists before submitting again. If sample agents fail to load, they remain optional; you can submit the application without selecting them.
