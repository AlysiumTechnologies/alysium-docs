<span id="price-your-agent" />

Set the monthly subscription price, included AI usage and free preview for your agent. You can also choose a usage multiplier for eligible usage beyond the included allowance.

A monthly fee buys access to the agent. **Premium Allowance** is the AI usage included with that subscription; it does not promise a fixed number of messages, because different requests can use different amounts of credits.

## Open Pack pricing

1. Open [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces) and select the source agent you want to price.
2. Select **Deploy**, then **Share & Sell**.
3. In **Buyer access**, select **Edit subscription setup** to open **Pack pricing**.

If the agent already has a live paid channel, saving a new price or allowance can apply the updated offer to new buyers immediately. Existing subscribers keep the pricing captured when they subscribed.

<span id="decide-what-the-offer-includes" />

## Enter and save pricing

1. Enter the recurring fee in **Monthly access price (USD)**.
2. Enter a positive whole number of credits in **Premium Allowance**. The form shows their dollar value at **100 credits = \$1.00**. That value must be strictly less than the monthly fee. For example, a \$10 monthly fee with 100 included credits supplies \$1 of included usage value.
3. Expand **Free preview messages** and set **Number of messages** to the number visitors can try before subscribing. Enter zero to offer no free preview messages. These messages use your credits, so choose a trial you are willing to fund.
4. Expand **Usage revenue** and review **Credit multiplier**. **No markup** uses standard rates. Moving the slider right, up to 5×, makes eligible additional usage consume more buyer credits. It does not increase the included allowance or monthly fee.
5. Select **Save**, wait for **Pricing saved** and check the saved Buyer access summary. Reopen **Edit subscription setup** to confirm the values if needed.

## Understand usage and previews

Included agent usage is used before additional usage from the buyer's Alysium space. Creator usage-margin earnings apply only when a Pro buyer uses the agent beyond its included allowance. Bundled usage, free preview messages and Free-plan buyers do not earn that extra margin. A multiplier above standard therefore does not mean every interaction earns additional revenue.

The buyer's space plan and usage limits still govern additional usage. Setting a larger multiplier does not give the buyer more credits. You can [read how earnings reach you](/selling-and-payouts/when-and-how-you-get-paid) separately from choosing the offer's price.

## Activate the intended buyer-access mode

Saving Pack pricing does not change **Free access** to **Paid subscription**.

1. Return to **Buyer access** in Share & Sell and select **Paid subscription**.
2. Read the confirmation, then select **Switch to paid**.
3. Wait for saving and check the selected buyer-access mode and the channel's displayed access.

To stop asking new buyers to subscribe, choose **Free access** and confirm **Switch to free**. That choice does not erase existing subscription obligations or earlier charges.

On an already-live channel, these confirmed mode changes can update live access as they save. For a new channel, complete its remaining readiness and activation:

- **Embedded widget:** check its pricing, buyer highlights and payment requirements. Complete any named blocker, then use the offered publish or update action. **Current widget access** shows the resulting mode. Installing the widget on a website is a separate distribution step.
- **AgentHub:** complete the listing and creator-application requirements, then use **Submit to AgentHub**, or the update/resubmit action shown for an existing submission. Check the resulting review state. AgentHub review is separate from selling through your own widget or standalone link.

Paid readiness also needs buyer highlights and Stripe. On **Widget purchase modal**, select **Edit** and use three highlights. Use **Add highlight** or an expanded highlight's **Remove highlight** control to adjust the count. Expand each highlight, fill **Headline** and **Supporting text**, then select **Save**. For payment setup, open [Team settings](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=team) → **Creator** → **Setup**. Use the offered Stripe onboarding action, complete Stripe's requested information, return and scroll to **Refresh Stripe status**. Read the resulting status; a saved price alone does not confirm payment readiness. You can [finish payment setup](/selling-and-payouts/set-up-stripe-to-get-paid) or [prepare the AgentHub listing](/selling-and-payouts/list-your-agent-on-agenthub) when that is your chosen route.

## If pricing cannot save or selling is blocked

Correct the displayed field message. In particular, the Premium Allowance must be positive and its value must be less than the monthly price; an equal value is invalid too.

If saving fails, reopen the setup and check the saved values before retrying. Closing without saving can show an unsaved-changes confirmation; discarding those edits does not save them.

Read the intended channel's actual readiness and any **Channel update**, update or resubmit request. Address the named requirement and check the resulting status after the action. You can [compare the sharing and selling routes](/selling-and-payouts/ways-to-share-or-sell-your-agent) if you have not chosen how people will reach the offer.
