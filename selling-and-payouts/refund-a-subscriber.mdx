Choose one subscriber payment, request its eligible refund and check both the payment result and the subscriber's access afterward.

## Choose cancellation or refund

Use **Cancel subscription** when you want to stop renewal while keeping access through the paid period. This action is in **Manage** on the subscriber's detail page; review the period-end date in its confirmation before confirming cancellation.

A refund returns a payment. If a successful full refund covers the payment funding the current access period, the subscription ends immediately once Stripe confirms cancellation. Refunding an older payment does not necessarily end current access.

The in-app **Refund** action is for successful payments within the **30-day self-service window**. This window determines which payments you can refund here; it is separate from payout and bank timing. The action requests the payment's remaining refundable amount, with no amount field for choosing an arbitrary partial refund.

## Open the subscriber

1. Open [Team settings](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=team), which opens the current space's settings.
2. Select **Creator**, then **Subscribers**.
3. Open the intended subscriber and check the buyer and agent names.
4. Scroll to **Payment history**.

Payment history shows recent payments, rather than a complete billing archive. Use the payment's date, amount and status to identify the charge you intend to return. For example, return the current month's payment by choosing that month's charge, even if an older charge has the same amount.

## Review the intended charge

1. Select **Refund** beside the intended payment.
2. In **Refund and end access?**, check the amount, charge date and buyer. Read the condition about ending current-period access.
3. Select **Refund & end access** to submit, or **Keep as-is** to leave the payment unchanged.
4. Wait for the action to finish, then read the refreshed payment history and the message near **Manage**.

A failed charge has nothing to refund. **Refund** is also disabled for an already refunded payment, a refund already submitted in this view, or while another action is running. Older payments direct you to support. If the intended payment or amount cannot be handled by this action, contact [billing@alysium.ai](mailto:billing@alysium.ai) with its reference, date and amount.

## Recognize a confirmed or pending result

Read the result message together with the payment and subscription statuses:

| Result | What to do |
|---|---|
| The refund and cancellation are confirmed | Check that the payment is refunded and the message confirms access has ended. |
| The refund or cancellation is still being verified | Reload the subscriber detail when you need the latest result. Do not submit another refund for the same charge while verification continues. |
| The refund did not complete | Check the payment status. The subscription remains unchanged. |
| Cancellation needs review, or the payment and access results disagree | Keep the payment reference and displayed message, and contact billing@alysium.ai before taking another action. |

A payment can still display **Paid** while a submitted refund is being verified. That is not a reason to send a second request. If the action reports an error, check the refreshed records before deciding what to do next.

The refund concerns subscription earnings; already-earned usage margin remains separate. Refund confirmation also does not establish when the buyer's bank will show the money. Your own [earnings and payout timing](/selling-and-payouts/when-and-how-you-get-paid) follows separate stages.
