Use **Creator** to follow your space's selling activity, find a paid subscriber, review an offer or check payment setup. [Analytics](/analytics/read-your-analytics-dashboard) is the separate view for conversations and feedback.

## Open the dashboard

Open [Team settings](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=team), which opens your active space's settings, then select **Creator**. Check the space name before opening its business records. You do not need AgentHub application approval to use Creator.

Choose a view beneath the Creator heading. On a narrow screen, scroll that row sideways to reach the other views.

## Choose the view for your question

| View | What you can find there |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Revenue, active subscribers, pending payout information, top listings and recent activity. Use the period selector on **Revenue** to compare **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Month to date** or **Previous month**. |
| **Subscribers** | Paid subscriptions across your listings. Search by name, email or agent, or filter to **All**, **Active**, **Canceling** or **Canceled**. Select a buyer's row to open the subscription and payment history. |
| **Listings** | Your offers and their performance. Select a listing to inspect its subscribers, pricing and usage information. |
| **Payouts** | Pending Alysium usage-earnings transfers and their history. Subscription payments and bank payouts are separate records in Stripe. |
| **Setup** | Stripe payment readiness, bank and tax setup progress, and the first-listing step. |

In Overview, use **View** on **Active subscribers** or **Pending payout** to open the corresponding view. You can also select a listing under **Top listings** or a buyer under **Top buyers** to open that record.

If Creator shows only **Overview** and **Setup**, open Setup and complete its payment-setup actions. If a previously completed setup needs a fresh payment check, the business views can remain available with a notice directing you to refresh Stripe status in Setup.

## Act from the right record

For a subscription question, open **Subscribers** and select the buyer. Check both the buyer and agent name. The status and date show whether the subscription is **Active** and renews, **Canceling** and ends at the period boundary, or **Canceled** and has ended. Check **Payment history** for the individual charge rather than using the subscription label as proof that a payment succeeded.

**Cancel subscription** stops renewal while keeping access through the paid period. A refund is an action on a particular payment and can end current access sooner. [Review the charge and its access effect](/selling-and-payouts/refund-a-subscriber) before requesting a refund.

For an offer change, open **Listings**, select the agent and choose **Edit agent** or **Edit pricing**. Edit agent opens its builder. Edit pricing opens **Deploy**; select **Share & Sell** there to reach the [subscription setup](/selling-and-payouts/price-your-agent).

For bank, tax or bank-payout details, open **Setup** and use **Open Stripe Express** when offered for your linked Express account. Use the actual Stripe payout record to check expected bank arrival; an Overview revenue total or dashboard date is not a bank receipt. You can [check the separate payment stages and waiting periods](/selling-and-payouts/when-and-how-you-get-paid) when comparing those records.

## If a view does not load

If a section says **Couldn't load overview**, **Couldn't load subscribers**, **Couldn't load listings** or **Couldn't load payouts**, reopen or reload that section and read the resulting message. A loading placeholder or an empty section beside a load error does not confirm zero activity.

For an unfinished payment setup, open **Setup**, complete the action offered and check the resulting status. The [Stripe setup steps](/selling-and-payouts/set-up-stripe-to-get-paid) explain how to return from Stripe and refresh that result.
