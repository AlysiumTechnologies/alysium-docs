Choose two things: where people reach your agent, and whether they need a paid subscription. Distribution and buyer access are separate.

Saving a price does not install a website widget or make an AgentHub listing discoverable. Use an agent whose selling setup you can manage.

## Choose a distribution route

Open [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces), select the intended agent and open **Deploy**. Home opens your active space; choose your own agent there.

| Route | Use it when | Continue with |
|---|---|---|
| **Share link + QR** | You can send people directly to the standalone chat. | [Share your agent with a link and QR code](/share-and-deploy/share-your-agent-with-a-link-and-qr-code). |
| **Embed on a website** | You own or manage a website and can install and publish its code. | [Put your agent on your website](/share-and-deploy/put-your-agent-on-your-website). |
| **Share & Sell → AgentHub** | You want the agent considered for the discoverable storefront. | [Apply to sell on Agenthub](/selling-and-payouts/apply-to-sell-on-agenthub) if needed, then [List your agent on Agenthub](/selling-and-payouts/list-your-agent-on-agenthub). |

AgentHub creator/listing review is separate from selling through a link or widget. **Share & Sell** also contains buyer-access and selling setup for your offer.

## Choose free or paid buyer access

Under **Share & Sell → Buyer access**, **Free access** and **Paid subscription** determine whether people are asked to subscribe. Switching opens a confirmation; read it before changing an agent people already use.

- **Switch to free** stops asking new buyers to subscribe after the change saves. It does not establish that earlier bills were erased.
- **Switch to paid** requires completed saved subscription setup before paid access can work. Follow [Price your agent](/selling-and-payouts/price-your-agent), including the separate mode confirmation and result check.

For example, a fictional business could keep a FAQ agent free for website visitors and make a separate specialist coaching agent a paid offer. These are two example purposes, not a recommendation that every agent should charge.

## Complete the readiness for your route

Review the agent's real facts and instructions before sharing it. [Make your agent live](/share-and-deploy/make-your-agent-live) handles publication and public access.

For paid subscriptions, complete [Stripe setup](/selling-and-payouts/set-up-stripe-to-get-paid) and [pricing](/selling-and-payouts/price-your-agent), then check the intended channel's saved readiness. Finish the chosen distribution guide and check its actual generated destination or installed widget.

Payment readiness, publication and AgentHub approval remain separate. If a channel shows a blocker, follow its named requirement. Changing another channel does not clear it automatically.
