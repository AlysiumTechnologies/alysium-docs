<span id="when-and-how-you-get-paid" />

Your agent's subscription payments and eligible usage earnings reach you through different steps. A payment from a buyer, an Alysium transfer and a deposit into your bank are separate records, with separate waiting periods.

| Money or movement | What it represents |
|---|---|
| **Subscription payment** | A buyer's payment for access to your agent, received in your connected Stripe account. |
| **Usage earnings** | Separate revenue from eligible additional AI usage. |
| **Alysium usage transfer** | Eligible usage earnings moved to your connected Stripe account. |
| **Bank payout** | Money paid from the Stripe account to your bank. |

Creator's **Payout history** records Alysium usage transfers and earlier payout records. Use Stripe for subscription receipts and bank-payout details. Seeing the same usage transfer in both places does not make it a second earning.

## Understand the waiting periods

**Each successful subscription payment has a settlement delay of 30 US business days.** This applies to renewals as well as the first payment. Once funds settle, standard automatic payouts follow a daily schedule. The expected bank-arrival date belongs to the actual Stripe payout.

A seller account's age does not shorten a new payment's delay. For example, a renewal received after your first payout starts its own settlement period.

**Usage earnings have a separate 30-calendar-day age rule before an Alysium transfer.** Eligible earnings must also reach the account's minimum transfer amount. Smaller eligible amounts keep accumulating until that minimum is reached, so reaching 30 days alone does not promise a transfer that day.

Payment requirements or an account restriction can also affect availability. You can [check payment readiness and the action it needs](/selling-and-payouts/what-your-stripe-status-means) in Creator's Setup view.

## Check the right screens

1. Open [Team settings](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=team), which opens the active space's settings, then select **Creator → Payouts**.
2. Read **Next payout** for the pending Alysium usage-transfer amount. Its **Earliest** date is not an expected bank-arrival date; check the actual Stripe payout for that.
3. In **Payout history**, match the record's **Date**, **Period**, **Status** and **Amount**. On a narrow screen, the period appears beneath the date. Use a wider view to see the Status column.
4. For your linked Stripe Express account, select **Setup**, then **Open Stripe Express**. In Stripe, inspect the relevant subscription payment, balance or bank payout. Match its amount, date and reference to the item you are checking, and use its recorded availability and expected-arrival dates.

If Creator shows only **Overview** and **Setup**, open **Setup** and complete the remaining payment steps. The [Stripe setup procedure](/selling-and-payouts/set-up-stripe-to-get-paid) explains the onboarding and status check.

## Read the displayed status

These labels describe the Alysium history item:

| Status | What you can conclude |
|---|---|
| **In transit** | The usage transfer is queued or processing. Check Stripe for its actual movement and any later bank payout. |
| **Paid** | The recorded history item is complete. Confirm the separate bank payout in Stripe when checking a deposit. |
| **Failed** | The item did not complete successfully. Check the relevant Stripe account and transfer information before deciding what happened. |
| **No payouts yet** | No payout history is currently displayed. A separate subscription receipt can still exist in Stripe. |

If **Couldn't load payouts** appears, reload or reopen the section before relying on an empty history or total. For a continuing load failure, failed item or unexpected amount, email [billing@alysium.ai](mailto:billing@alysium.ai) with the space name, visible reference, date, amount and status.

For a bank deposit, compare Stripe's payout result with your bank record. The end of a settlement period and a pending transfer total do not establish that the bank has received the money.
