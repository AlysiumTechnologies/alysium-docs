<span id="change-a-member-role" />

You cannot currently change another person's role in an Alysium space. Each space contains only its owner; adding people and assigning member roles are unavailable. You also cannot transfer ownership through the Team controls.

## Match the access need to the right task

If someone needs to use your agent, you can [share its chat with a link](/share-and-deploy/share-your-agent-with-a-link-and-qr-code). They can chat under the agent's sharing settings without becoming a space member or gaining permission to edit its instructions or files.

Working together to edit agents or manage the same space is not currently available. A Free or Pro plan does not add members to your space.

## Ask about a specific access problem

For an ownership question or an access problem, email [support@alysium.ai](mailto:support@alysium.ai). Include the space, what you want to do and any message you see.
