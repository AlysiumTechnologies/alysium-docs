<span id="invite-people-to-your-space" />

You cannot currently invite other people to an Alysium space. Each space contains only its owner. In [Team settings](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=team), **Invite People** is disabled.

## Share an agent for chatting

If someone needs to use your agent, you can [share its chat with a link or QR code](/share-and-deploy/share-your-agent-with-a-link-and-qr-code). This gives them a way to chat under the agent's sharing settings. It does not add them to your space or let them edit its instructions or files.

## Ask about editing or management needs

Shared editing and space management are not currently available through member invitations. To discuss work you need to do with another person, email [support@alysium.ai](mailto:support@alysium.ai) with the space and the tasks you want to share.
