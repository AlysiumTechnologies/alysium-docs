<span id="leave-a-space" />

You cannot currently leave your own Alysium space. Each space contains only its owner, and the Team controls do not offer a working way to remove yourself or transfer ownership.

## End a session or use another account

To end your browser session, open your avatar menu at the bottom of the sidebar and select **Log Out**. Your space and account remain saved.

If you intend to use a different account, you can [sign in with that account](/getting-started/sign-in-to-alysium) afterward.

## Ask about account or data deletion

If you want your account or data removed, [request account or data deletion](/getting-started/delete-your-account-or-your-data). Logging out keeps your account and data.

## Resolve an unexpected space or access problem

[Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces) opens your saved active space. There is currently no space picker in the sidebar or avatar menu. If the work shown is unexpected, check the account in your avatar menu.

For help finding your work or resolving an ownership problem, email [support@alysium.ai](mailto:support@alysium.ai) with the space or work you expected and what you see.
