There is no current member-removal task to complete in an Alysium space. A space contains only its owner, invitations and member-role workflows are unavailable, and the owner cannot be removed.

Space membership, personal account deletion and access to an agent's chat are separate matters. The current experience does not provide an additional member to select and remove.

## If you mean access to an agent's chat

Someone who opens a public agent link is using that agent's visitor chat. That does not make them a member of your space.

Review the agent's sharing settings using [Share your agent with a link and QR code](/share-and-deploy/share-your-agent-with-a-link-and-qr-code) or [Share a chat link with someone](/share-and-deploy/share-a-chat-link-with-someone). Those guides explain the supported link controls and their audiences. Member removal is not a substitute for reviewing the access route that person uses.

Public or separately granted chat access has its own rules. If the access route is unclear, ask for help before treating it as changed.

## If you mean ownership or account access

The owner cannot be removed from their personal space. Ownership cannot be transferred through the role menu. Use [Get help from a person](/help/get-help-from-a-person) for an account-specific question. Include the space and the access you intend to change, without passwords or verification codes.

A support request is not confirmation that access was revoked. [Invite people to your space](/spaces/invite-people-to-your-space) explains the current invitation limit; removing and reinviting someone is not an available workaround.
