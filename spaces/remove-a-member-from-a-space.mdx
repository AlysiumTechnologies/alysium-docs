<span id="remove-a-member-from-a-space" />

You cannot currently remove a member from an Alysium space. Each space contains only its owner; invitations and workflows for adding or managing other members are unavailable.

<span id="if-you-mean-access-to-an-agent’s-chat" />
<span id="if-you-mean-access-to-an-agents-chat" />

## Agent chat access

Someone using your agent through a shared link is a chat visitor, not a member of your space. To change how visitors reach the agent, review its [link-sharing settings](/share-and-deploy/share-your-agent-with-a-link-and-qr-code) or its separate [Chat sharing control](/share-and-deploy/share-a-chat-link-with-someone).

If you are unsure how someone has access, email [support@alysium.ai](mailto:support@alysium.ai) with the agent's name and the type of link they use.

<span id="if-you-mean-ownership-or-account-access" />

## Ownership and account data

The owner cannot remove themselves from their personal space or transfer ownership through the Team controls. For an ownership or account-access problem, email support@alysium.ai with the space and the access you need to change.

If your goal is to erase your account or personal data, you can [make a deletion request](/getting-started/delete-your-account-or-your-data) separately.
