Alysium keeps track of your account's active space. The current sidebar and avatar menu have no space-switching picker. Opening Home returns to the active space; it does not select a different one.

A space currently contains only its owner. Invitations and member-role workflows are unavailable, so switching spaces is not a way to join another person's team.

## What the navigation entries open

| Entry | Current behavior |
|---|---|
| [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces) | Opens the named account's active space and its agents. |
| [Team settings](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=team) | Opens the Team tab for that active space. It does not choose another space. |
| Sidebar or avatar menu | Offers navigation and account controls, without a space picker. |

[What a space is](/getting-started/what-a-space-is) explains the distinction between your account, its space and the agents inside it.

## If the expected work is missing

Check which account you are using and review the agents on Home before editing or creating duplicates. For example, a fictional workshop owner using a different personal login may see different work.

If you already have an authorized destination for the space you need, use that actual destination and confirm the displayed work. Do not invent an address or alter another person's link. A public agent-chat link does not grant space membership.

Without an authorized destination, use [Get help from a person](/help/get-help-from-a-person). Explain the account, the space or work you expected, and what you currently see. The request does not itself switch spaces or grant access.

## If you intend to change accounts

Use [Log out](/getting-started/log-out), then [Sign in to Alysium](/getting-started/sign-in-to-alysium), only when you intend to use another account. Review guest-session limits before logging out of a temporary session.

Changing accounts changes your identity. It does not grant that account membership in someone else's space or add a space-picker control.
