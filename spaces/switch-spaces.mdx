<span id="switch-spaces" />

Alysium opens your saved active space when you select Home. There is currently no space picker in the sidebar or avatar menu, so those menus do not offer a way to choose a different space.

A space currently contains only its owner. Invitations and member-role workflows are unavailable.

## What the navigation entries open

| Entry | Where it takes you |
|---|---|
| [Home](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces) | Your active space and its agents. |
| [Team settings](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=team) | The Team tab for that active space. |

Neither entry selects another space. Alysium's [account and space concepts](/getting-started/what-a-space-is) explain how your identity relates to the work inside a space.

## If the expected work is missing

Open your avatar menu at the bottom of the sidebar and check which account you are using. Review the agents on Home.

If you are using the intended account and still cannot find the expected space or work, email [support@alysium.ai](mailto:support@alysium.ai). Include the space or agent names you expected and what you currently see.

## If you intend to change accounts

Open your avatar menu and select **Log Out**, then sign in with the account whose work you want to open. You can [check the sign-in options](/getting-started/sign-in-to-alysium) if needed.

Logging out ends this browser session without deleting your space. Changing accounts opens that account's work; it does not choose a different space for the same account.
