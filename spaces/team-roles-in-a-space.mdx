An Alysium space currently contains one person: its owner. You do not need to configure a team to build agents or manage your space.

## Role terms and current availability

**Owner** means the account that owns the space. Open [Team settings](https://app.alysium.ai/spaces?settings=team) to see the owner of your active space.

**Admin**, **Editor** and **Viewer** are member-role names, but adding people and assigning or changing their roles are currently unavailable. **Invite People** is disabled. The owner cannot leave or be removed through the Team controls, and those controls do not offer ownership transfer.

## Roles are separate from plans and agent access

The [Free and Pro plans](/plans-and-billing/free-and-pro-plans-compared) concern a space's features and usage. Neither plan adds members.

Adding or subscribing to another creator's agent gives you access under that offer, rather than membership in the creator's space. An acquired agent may be for chatting without builder editing; owning your space does not automatically make it editable.

You can [share your agent for chatting](/share-and-deploy/share-your-agent-with-a-link-and-qr-code) without making visitors members or editors of your space.

For an ownership or access question, email [support@alysium.ai](mailto:support@alysium.ai) with the space and the work you need to do.
