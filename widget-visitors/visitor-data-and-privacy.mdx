The agent's creator can read saved conversations with that agent. Before sending personal material, review the agent's privacy information and understand the separate choices for conversation history, uploaded files and data requests.

## Find the information for the agent you use

Open the widget on the website where you use the agent. If a conversation is open, select **Back to browse**, then select **Account** in the bottom navigation. Open **Data & Privacy**.

Review **AI Model Information** and **What's Shared With Creator**, along with the policy links. These help you understand the agent you are using; read the applicable policies for details about how your data is handled. **About This Agent** in Account provides agent and creator information. Account may also have **Terms & Privacy** when the creator has supplied a terms link.

Give the agent the information needed for your request. For example, you can often ask what a returns policy allows without supplying a customer's name, account number or full order document.

## History, uploaded files and data requests are different

| Choice or action | What it changes |
|---|---|
| **Conversation history** | Whether future messages are added to your saved history with this agent. It does not erase earlier chats or files. |
| Delete a file in **Your uploaded files** | Removes that upload and its copy shared with the creator when deletion completes. |
| Contact the responsible creator or team | Starts a separate request about your account or data. Changing history does not submit that request. |

Turning history off controls saved conversation history. It does not mean that no other records are kept while the service handles your messages and generates replies.

### Change future message saving

Connected guests and signed-in visitors can both change this preference. It applies to you and this agent.

1. Open **Account** and find **Conversation history**.
2. Wait for the preference to load. Turn it off to stop saving future messages to history, or on to save them for later.
3. Wait for the small saving spinner to finish. Your choice saves automatically.
4. Leave Account, reopen it and wait for the preference to load again. Check that the switch still shows your choice.

If it returns to its earlier state, reload the website widget, reopen Account and check again. Until that check confirms the new choice, do not treat it as saved. Turning history back on does not recover messages that were not saved. An account can help you [return to saved conversations](/widget-visitors/visitor-accounts-and-saved-history), while older chats and files need separate attention if you want them removed.

### Read upload notices separately

The first upload for an agent in a session shows a **Privacy Notice**. Read it before selecting **Upload**. It explains that AI will process the file to answer you and warns against uploading sensitive personal information such as identity documents, financial data or passwords. If creator sharing is enabled, the notice also explains that the creator can see the file, keeps a copy and may use it as agent knowledge. Select **Cancel** if you do not want to proceed.

Turning history off does not delete files you already uploaded. To remove a stored upload:

1. Open **Account** → **Your uploaded files**.
2. Open the file's menu and select **Delete**.
3. Read the confirmation. Deletion also removes the copy shared with the creator and cannot be undone. Select **Delete** to confirm, or **Keep** to cancel.
4. Wait for **File deleted** before treating the removal as complete. If the card says **Couldn't delete.**, use **Try again** and check the result.

Deleting a file does not erase the conversation or retract an answer someone has already read. You can [review file types, upload limits and the creator's copy](/widget-visitors/files-visitors-send-you) when deciding what to upload.

### Ask for account or data help

In **Account**, select **Help & Support**. It opens either the creator's help page in a new tab or a screen with the configured contact details.

If that screen supplies an email contact, use **Copy** beside it. **Copied** means the address is on your clipboard; paste it into your own email app and send your request there. Copying the address does not send a request.

Explain which agent and account your request concerns, and whether you want access to data, removal of particular material or account deletion. For a broader removal request, [choose the scope of account or data deletion](/getting-started/delete-your-account-or-your-data). Follow the responsible team's response and wait for its confirmation before treating the request as complete.
